# PART I — STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

## 1. Vision

ODG BTP International must become an intelligent operating ecosystem for construction businesses. It must not remain a quotation application, a basic invoicing tool, a chatbot or a collection of disconnected software modules. Its purpose is to help construction companies manage and improve their entire economic and operational lifecycle.

The target lifecycle is: **Market opportunity → Customer acquisition → Qualification → Site analysis → Measurement → Quotation → Contract → Planning → Procurement → Execution → Monitoring → Invoicing → Payment → Profitability → Learning.**

The system must progressively understand a construction business, its activities, projects and constraints; identify opportunities and risks; prepare operational decisions; execute permitted deterministic tasks; request human confirmation when required; measure the outcome of its recommendations; learn from validated results; and improve future decisions. The final objective is to help each customer run a more efficient and profitable construction business.

## 2. The strategic ambition

Develop a product with demonstrable superiority in selected measurable dimensions. Do not claim that no competitor has a similar feature without exhaustive evidence. Build advantage through the combination of: (1) end-to-end construction intelligence; (2) continuous financial and operational feedback; (3) multimodal field intelligence; (4) cross-métier knowledge; (5) country-specific regulatory intelligence; (6) integrated commercial intelligence; (7) AI-assisted execution with human authorization; (8) a partner and supplier ecosystem; (9) measurable customer outcomes; (10) a continuously improving knowledge and evidence system. The competitive ambition is to win through the quality of the complete customer outcome, not the number of features.

## 3. Canonical integration into V12.4

This roadmap is an additive extension of the existing canonical roadmap. Target file: `docs/odg/v124-consolidated-roadmap.json`. Master specification: `ODG_BTP_WORLD_CLASS_V12_4_ROADMAP.md`.

## 11. New acquisition architecture — five independent engines
- **Engine A — Partner acquisition:** material suppliers, distributors, trade associations, accountants, training orgs, consultants, insurance/professional-service partners, software integrators. Proposition: qualified referrals, demo accounts, training, revenue-sharing where legally appropriate, co-branded campaigns, partner dashboards, performance tracking. - **Engine B — Customer referrals:** measurable system; rewards (subscription credit, modules, upgrades, partner benefits) conditional on valid paid customers, not clicks/registrations. Measure invitations, accepted, paying referred, referral CAC, referral retention, fraud/duplicate detection.

Demos must use real working functionality or be explicitly identified as prototypes. Build self-service demo, guided demo, interactive tour, trial onboarding, automated follow-up.

## 29. CAC, LTV and payback
CAC = fully loaded acquisition expenses / new paying customers. LTV = average recurring revenue × gross margin / monthly churn. Planning assumptions: ARPA €134 at maturity; gross margin 70%; monthly churn 3%. Indicative LTV: €134 × 70% / 3% ≈ €3,127. At fully-loaded CAC €250: LTV/CAC ≈ 12.5×. Gross-profit payback: €250 / (€134 × 70%) ≈ 2.7 months. These are ambitious internal targets and must not be represented as achieved. The 2025 B2B-SaaS benchmark research reports a median CAC payback around 16 months in one large sample; validate against ODG's actual sales motion, pricing and costs.

## 33. Partner growth flywheel
Supplier/partner joins → tracked referral link → prospect registers → prospect activates → customer pays → partner receives agreed benefit → partner refers more. Measure active partners, qualified referrals, paid conversion, partner CAC, revenue per partner, retention of referred customers.

## 34. Customer growth flywheel
Customer receives value → validates result → shares recommendation → new customer joins → activates → network grows. Transparent referral rewards; do not reward fraudulent/duplicate/non-paying registrations.

## 35. Organic growth flywheel
Useful content → qualified organic traffic → calculator/diagnostic → registration → activation → customer outcome → validated case study → more content.

## 36. Advertising stop and scale rules
Stop/reduce when: clicks without qualified leads; leads without activation; CAC exceeds the approved ceiling; poor retention; insufficient product readiness; unsupported claims. Scale only when: paid customers verified; conversion repeatable; gross margin understood; retention acceptable; CAC payback measurable.
