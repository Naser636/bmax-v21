## Method
Per scene: (dialogue cues × seconds/cue) + (action words × seconds/word), with **staged FLOORS** on the ~16
set-piece/montage scenes whose screen time is action-driven (storm, seam, market tide, canyon, Helgi's death,
siege, seal interior, the wordless montage, joint evac, etc.). Low rate = terse direction (10 s/cue, 0.30
s/action-word); High rate = suspense-staged (18 s/cue, 0.52 s/action-word; floors ×1.4).

## Conclusion (honest)
**Yes — 60:00 is reachable from F7 without artificial filling.** The screenplay, timed scene-by-scene at
realistic staged rates, already estimates feature-length (low 56 / high 91). No new scenes are needed to reach

measured 60:00 cut (that requires produced footage). So: **length is supported by a scene-timing model; it is not

   the band.
4. **Target in the edit:** 60:00 ± 2:00; the matrix (`23`) target durations are the per-scene budget the editor
   cuts to.
