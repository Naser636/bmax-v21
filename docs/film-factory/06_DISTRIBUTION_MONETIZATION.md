# Distribution, Audience & Monetization — ODG_FILM_FACTORY_V1 (addendum §7/§9)

Strategy only; no asset published; no views/revenue guaranteed. Prepared against platform rules + AI-disclosure
duties (verify current policy before release).

## Release package (to produce once a master exists — NOT yet)
- **Title/thumbnail/promise aligned** (no clickbait that the film can't pay off — a strong film is the real lever).
- **Trailer (~60–90s) + 3–5 vertical teasers** (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) cut from the hero beats (water→sand).
- **Subtitles** (FR/EN first) + a **dubbing plan** (ElevenLabs multilingual) for EN/ES/DE/AR expansion.
- **SEO/metadata**: title, description, chapters, tags; thumbnail A/B variants.
- **AI-disclosure label** on the asset + description (platform synthetic-content rules) — mandatory.

## Channel plan
- **YouTube** primary (discovery + retention economics + monetization after eligibility — thresholds vary, verify).
- Festival/short-film circuit if the proof-of-concept short lands creatively (credibility > ad views).
- Vertical shorts as the discovery funnel to the long piece.

## Measurement loop (post-publish; NOT_APPLICABLE until live)
CTR on thumbnail/title, average-view-duration/retention curve, traffic sources, swipe-through on verticals,
sub-conversion; iterate titles/thumbnails/teasers from real data. No metric is projected as a guarantee.

## Monetization (scenarios, not promises)
Ad revenue (post-eligibility), sponsorship, licensing of the short, and — honestly — the most realistic early
value is **portfolio/credibility** (proof the pipeline works) rather than direct revenue. Separate **views ≠
monetized views ≠ profit**; no revenue figure is committed.

## Rights/compliance gate (before any release)
Commercial-use licences for every tool output (prefer Runway/Veo/MJ/ElevenLabs/Suno-paid; avoid Kling right-to-
use + Udio no-export for distributable work); music rights (ElevenLabs Music v2 / Suno commercial); font/image
licences; voice/likeness permissions; platform T&Cs; AI-disclosure. Maintain a provenance+licence register per
asset (reuse ODG `evidence-provenance`).
