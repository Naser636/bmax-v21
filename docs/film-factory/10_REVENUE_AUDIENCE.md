# Revenue & Audience Model — ODG_FILM_FACTORY_V1 (override V2 §5/§6)

Explicit, assumption-driven, NOT a forecast of this specific film. No guarantee of views, monetization approval,
or profit. Separates total views / monetized views / subscribers+watch-hours / ad revenue / sponsorship-licensing.

## Assumptions (public benchmarks; verify; this film is unproven)
- YouTube Partner Program eligibility (commonly cited): ~1,000 subscribers + ~4,000 public watch-hours /12 mo (or
  a Shorts-views equivalent) — **verify current policy**. Ads only on *monetized* playbacks after approval.
- RPM (revenue/1,000 monetized views) for narrative/film content: wide; use **€1 (low) / €3 (central) / €6
  (high)**. Monetized views ≈ **40–70%** of total views.
- CTR on impressions 4–8%; retention is the real driver of reco traffic; geography shifts RPM heavily.

## Break-even (ad revenue only)
| Spend | Monetized views to break even @ €1 / €3 / €6 RPM |
|---|---|
| Recommended **€2,000** | 2,000,000 / **666,667** / 333,333 |
| Ceiling **€4,000** | 4,000,000 / 1,333,333 / 666,667 |
⇒ Even at the recommended spend, ad-only break-even needs **hundreds of thousands to millions of monetized
views** — unlikely for a single short on a new channel. **Honest:** direct ad profit from one short is improbable;
the credible value is **portfolio/credibility + a channel/funnel** that compounds across releases.

## Scenarios (single short, new/small channel) — total views → ad revenue
| | 30 days | 90 days | 365 days | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 2k–10k views | 10k–40k | 40k–150k | likely below YPP → **€0** monetized; value = proof/portfolio |
| **Central** | 10k–50k | 50k–250k | 250k–1M | YPP reached mid-year; ad revenue ≈ **€150–2,000** (RPM-dep.) |
| **Optimistic** | 50k–300k | 300k–1.5M | 1M–5M | a breakout short; ad ≈ **€1,000–15,000** + possible sponsorship/licensing |
Assumptions per scenario: impressions × CTR 4–8% × retention-driven reco multiplier × monetized-fraction 40–70%
× RPM €1–6. All ranges are illustrative, not predictions.

## Max sensible paid promotion under the cap
≤ **€100–300** (from the reserve), used only to seed early retention signal — **NOT** counted as organic demand
and **NOT** as qualified monetization (platform excludes ad-driven views from some eligibility). Stop-rule: if
paid traffic retention < organic, halt promo.

## Stop / pivot triggers
- 30-day central-case miss (e.g., <10k views, <2% CTR, <30% retention) → stop promo, iterate title/thumbnail,
  treat as a portfolio piece, apply learnings to the next short.
- Negative unit economics on promo (CAC > plausible RPM yield) → halt immediately.

## Audience strategy (genuine value, not clickbait)
- **Hook:** the water→seam→two-suns cold open is the thumbnail+first-10s promise.
- **Title/thumbnail A/B concepts (to test):** (1) "A Viking Ship Washes Up in Ancient Egypt" + the twin-sun shore;
  (2) "She Had to Choose: Home, or Both Worlds" + Astrid at the seam; (3) "La Porte du Soleil | fantasy short" +
  the water-to-sand morph. Test CTR; never mislead vs the film.
- **Distribution:** the short + a 60–90s trailer + 3–5 vertical teasers (the hero beats) + FR/EN subtitles;
  localized dubs (ElevenLabs) only if early retention justifies the cost.
- **Compliance (mandatory):** YouTube original/authentic-content + repetitive/mass-produced-content policies;
  **AI-disclosure** label for realistic synthetic media; music/voice/image rights cleared (prefer ownership-clear
  tools); no artificial views/engagement/misleading metadata.
