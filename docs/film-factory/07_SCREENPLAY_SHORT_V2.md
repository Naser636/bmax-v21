# LA PORTE DU SOLEIL — proof-of-concept short screenplay (Draft V2.1)

> **V2.1 fixes (from 2nd independent critique, 6/10):** cut the climax double-thesis line; deleted the duplicated
> portal-rule exposition and replaced it with an action beat (Astrid *reads* the seam before it moves);
> reconciled the cosmology (alignment makes the seam possible; Meryt's rite tore it open early+wide); Nefer-Ka
> now yields on demonstrated evidence, not concession. Rev-1 design + both critiques preserved as history.

**ODG_FILM_FACTORY_V1 · 2026-10-24 · ~10–12 min · ORIGINAL fantasy-adventure.** Draft V2 incorporates the six
structural pivots (`02` §6) from the independent critique (`05`). The design doc + critique are preserved as
immutable history. This is a SCREENPLAY (text) — **not a film**. Creative writing = bounded provider delegation;
ODG verifies structure (`checks` below). Natural dialogue + subtext targeted; no opening proverb.

**Historical note:** original fantasy. Pharaonic-Egyptian and Norse elements are *dramatized*; the ~2,000-yr gap
is the premise (the alignment), not an error. Nothing depicts real historical persons or events.

**One-line:** Thrown through a seam between two ages onto an impossible Egyptian shore, a viking navigator who
has never once trusted her own judgment must make the first real choice of her life — and it is the one that
costs her the way home and her dying father both.

---

### 1. EXT. NORTH SEA — STORM — NIGHT
*No narration. Black water the size of hills. The longship SŌLFARI claws up a wave. At the steering-oar:
ASTRID (19), soaked, knuckles white — she is steering, but her eyes keep snapping to the deck below, to OLAF
(60s, grey, lashed upright to the mast, too weak to stand).*

**ASTRID:** Father — the swell's wrong, it's pulling us beam-on, do I hold or fall off?

**OLAF:** *(thin, barely heard)* ...You have the helm, girl.

**ASTRID:** I'm ASKING you —

**OLAF:** And I'm telling you you have it.

*She doesn't answer. She holds — the wrong choice; the wave takes them broadside. A wall of sea rises over the
rail. HELGI (20s, ties off a line beside a small carved toy — a child's horse) grabs her shoulder.*

**HELGI:** Astrid — whatever you're going to do, do it as if he's already gone. That's the only way you'll ever do it.

*The wave crests to break — and at its peak the WATER UNRAVELS. Not foam: a seam of hard gold LIGHT splits the
wave, and through the seam the sea becomes SAND, becomes dune, becomes a shore under a sky with TWO SUNS. The
ship drops through and grinds to a halt, keel in sand. Silence, total, after the roar.*

### 2. EXT. IMPOSSIBLE SHORE — DAWN OF TWO SUNS — CONTINUOUS
*The crew, a dozen, stunned. Behind the ship, the seam of light still hangs in the air over the sand — breathing,
widening a finger's width as they watch. A line of the river-guard crests the dune: MERYT (30s, scale-corslet,
khopesh), more rattled than she lets show. Behind her, a WATER-CARRIER — IPY (10), clay jug on her hip — who
should not be this close to a patrol.*

**MERYT:** *(Egyptian, subtitled)* Ipy. Behind me. Now.

**ASTRID:** *(empty hands, to her own men)* Oars down. Down. *(to Meryt, slow)* We didn't come here. The sea
opened and we fell through it.

*The seam PULSES. Twenty paces off, a stretch of dune shivers and — for three seconds — becomes seawater,
mirror-bright, then sand again. Both women flinch toward it at once. Ipy laughs at it, delighted, not afraid.*

**MERYT:** *(quietly, watching the seam, not Astrid)* It's widening.

**ASTRID:** You've seen it before.

**MERYT:** *(a beat too long)* No.
*She has. She's lying. She turns her horse before her face gives more away.*

### 3. EXT. GUARD CAMP, NILE-EDGE — DAY
*The ship dragged inland. Two suns bleach everything. NEFER-KA (30s, shaved head, a plain solar pectoral — worn,
not brandished) studies the hanging seam with a navigator's stillness that mirrors Astrid's. OLAF is laid in
shade, failing.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(to Astrid, not unkind)* You read water. I read this. *(nods at the seam)* It is an alignment —
it opens when the two suns touch, and every breath it stays open, one age bleeds into the other. The sea you
fell through is owed back. *(beat)* It does not close on its own.

**ASTRID:** Then how.

**NEFER-KA:** Someone reads it from the inside and closes it behind them. *(flat)* The seam does not give that
person back.

*ASTRID absorbs "from the inside." Behind Nefer-Ka, MERYT won't meet his eye — she knows more than she's said.
OLAF beckons Astrid close.*

**OLAF:** *(low, only to her)* I don't want to be buried in gold sand, Astrid. Chart us a way back into that
seam. Take me home to the grey.

**ASTRID:** *(the old reflex)* Tell me it's the right call and I'll do it.

**OLAF:** *(sad)* No. Not this time. *(he closes his eyes)* You'll choose it or you won't.

### 4. EXT. CAMP EDGE — THE SURGE — DAY
*The seam gives a deep TONE. The desync SURGES: a broad sheet of dune becomes SEA — fast, deep, roaring — right
where HELGI is hauling the ship's line, where IPY wandered chasing the earlier mirror. Water floods between them.*

**ASTRID:** HELGI —!

*HELGI is waist-deep, then chest, in a sea that shouldn't exist. He throws the child's wooden horse toward dry
sand — toward Astrid — not his hand, the toy, the thing that has to get home.*

**HELGI:** *(to Astrid, as it takes him)* As if he's already — !

*The sea-that-was-sand pulls him under and is gone — sand again, dry, flat, Helgi not there. The wooden horse
lies on the dune. IPY is stranded on a shrinking island of sand; MERYT crashes in and drags her clear, and in
the pull of it BLURTS —*

**MERYT:** *(to Nefer-Ka, breaking)* It was the dawn-rite. My guard lit the suns' touch at dawn. I opened it. I
opened it and I have been afraid to say so since the first ghost walked out of the sea.

*Silence. Nefer-Ka looks at her — not with rage, with grief.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(quiet)* The suns' touch only makes the seam *possible*. Your dawn-rite tore it open before its
time — and wide. *(beat)* So you already know what it costs to shut it.

### 5. EXT. BEFORE THE SEAM — DUSK, SUNS CONVERGING — LATER
*The seam is a tall wound of light now, close to the ship. NEFER-KA ties back his robe — preparing to walk into
it. He is the one who can read it; it is his age, his duty.*

**NEFER-KA:** My blood reads the suns. It should be me. *(to Meryt)* Tell them the guard held. Tell it better
than it happened.

*ASTRID steps between him and the seam. She doesn't argue. She kneels, sets Helgi's wooden horse in the sand, and
traces the seam's drift with two fingers — then points, flat, at a stretch of dune.*

**ASTRID:** There. Next breath.

*The seam PULSES — and the exact dune she pointed at ripples into sea and back. She read it before it moved.
NEFER-KA stares: she called it to the inch.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(quietly)* ...You read it.

**ASTRID:** I never once trusted the water under me. *(beat)* I'm done not trusting it. *(a look at the seam)*
Your world needs its guardian standing on this shore. Not lost in that light.

*Her hand stays on the wooden horse. She does not say the rest — that this is the first thing she has ever chosen
without being told to. NEFER-KA looks from her hand, to the seam she just read, and steps back.*

### 6. EXT. BEFORE THE SEAM — CONTINUOUS
*She goes to OLAF. He is almost gone.*

**ASTRID:** I can send you home. The ship, the crew, the grey sea — through the seam before I shut it. *(beat)*
But I'd have to stay on this side to close it. I can't take you home AND close it. I can only do one.

**OLAF:** *(eyes opening, clear for one moment)* ...The sea takes what it's owed. *(he almost smiles)* Owe it
me, then. Not you. Close it, Astrid. Choose it.

*She holds his hand until it's still. She does not weep — not yet. She stands.*

**ASTRID:** *(to Meryt)* Put my father in your gold sand. Build a grey cairn over him so he knows I was here.

### 7. EXT. INSIDE THE SEAM / SHORE — THE CLOSE — NIGHT→SINGLE DAWN
*ASTRID steps into the wound of light. INTERCUT: the crew and the ship caught in the seam's pull — then RELEASED
backward through it, toward grey sea and storm and home, Helgi's horse clutched in a sailor's fist. From inside
the light ASTRID reads the seam like a chart — hands moving — and draws it CLOSED behind the departing ship.*

*The two suns slide apart. One sinks. The sea that flooded the desert drains back into nothing. A single, ordinary
sun rises on an ordinary shore.*

*ASTRID stands on the sand — this side, alone, alive, exiled from her own age forever. She has made one choice,
and it was the whole of her.*

### 8. EXT. THE CAIRN — SINGLE-SUN MORNING
*A grey-stone cairn on gold sand — wrong and right at once. MERYT sets Olaf's iron arm-ring on top. IPY places
the wooden horse beside it. ASTRID looks at the plain, single sun.*

**MERYT:** *(beside her, quiet)* What do your people call a navigator with no sea to cross?

**ASTRID:** *(a long beat; the first calm of her life)* Home, maybe. Eventually.

*She turns inland, toward the living world she chose over the one she lost. HOLD on the cairn, the ring, the
horse. CUT TO BLACK. TITLE: LA PORTE DU SOLEIL.*
