# LA PORTE DU SOLEIL — full 60-minute screenplay (Draft F1)

**ODG_FILM_FACTORY_V1 · 2026-10-24 · target ~60 min · ORIGINAL fantasy-adventure.** Expands the proof-of-concept
spine (`07`, gate-passed 7/10) across the `14` structure. This is a **first full draft** — a screenplay, NOT a
film; it requires an independent critique + a human read before production (quality NOT yet claimed). Natural
dialogue + subtext targeted; stylized aesthetic (per `14`). Original fantasy; Norse/Egyptian detail dramatized,
distinct from historical fact; no real persons.

Principals: **ASTRID** (19, viking navigator — flaw: follows, never chooses) · **OLAF** (60s, her father, the
dying captain) · **HELGI** (20s, crewmate, carries a child's wooden horse) · **TOVE** (20s, crew, Astrid's friend)
· **MERYT** (30s, Egyptian river-guard captain — secret: her dawn-rite tore the seam open) · **NEFER-KA** (30s,
guardian who can read/seal the seam) · **IPY** (10, water-carrier). The seam = a time-alignment (no temple).

# ACT I — ARRIVAL (0:00–15:00)

## SEQUENCE 1 — THE CROSSING

### 1. EXT. NORTH SEA — STORM — NIGHT
*Black water the size of hills. The longship SŌLFARI claws up a wave. At the steering-oar, ASTRID — soaked,
knuckles white — steers, but her eyes keep dropping to OLAF, lashed upright to the mast, too weak to stand.*

**ASTRID:** Father — she's pulling beam-on. Hold, or fall off?

**OLAF:** *(thin)* You have the helm.

**ASTRID:** I'm asking you —

**OLAF:** And I keep giving it back.

*She holds — wrong; the wave takes them broadside. HELGI lashes a line one-handed, a small carved horse tucked in
his belt. TOVE bails beside him.*

**HELGI:** Astrid — do it like he's already gone. It's the only way you'll ever do anything.

*The wave crests — and at its peak the WATER UNRAVELS: a seam of hard gold light splits it, and through the seam
the sea becomes SAND, becomes dune, under a sky with TWO SUNS. The ship drops, keel into sand, and grinds still.
Silence after the roar.*

### 2. EXT. IMPOSSIBLE SHORE — DAWN OF TWO SUNS — CONTINUOUS
*A dozen Norse, stunned. Behind the ship the seam still hangs in the air, breathing, a finger wider each minute.
A line of river-guard crests the dune: MERYT, khopesh drawn, and too close behind her, IPY with a clay jug.*

**MERYT:** *(Egyptian, subtitled)* Ipy. Behind me.

**ASTRID:** *(empty hands, to her men)* Oars down. *(to Meryt, slow)* We didn't come here. The sea opened and
dropped us through.

*The seam PULSES. Twenty paces off, dune becomes mirror-bright SEA for three seconds, then sand. Both women flinch
toward it at once. IPY laughs at it, unafraid.*

**MERYT:** *(watching the seam, not Astrid)* ...It's wider than this morning.

**ASTRID:** You've seen it before.

**MERYT:** *(a beat too long)* No.
*She turns her horse before her face says more.*

## SEQUENCE 2 — TWO CAPTAINS

### 3. EXT. GUARD CAMP, NILE-EDGE — DAY
*The ship dragged inland under two suns. NEFER-KA studies the hanging seam with a navigator's stillness that
mirrors Astrid's. OLAF laid in shade, grey.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(to Astrid)* You read water. I read this.

**ASTRID:** Then read it to me.

**NEFER-KA:** It's an alignment. The two suns touch, and the wall between their ages thins. The sea you fell
through is owed back. *(beat)* It does not close on its own.

**ASTRID:** How does it close.

**NEFER-KA:** Someone reads it from the inside and shuts it behind them. *(flat)* The seam keeps that someone.

*ASTRID files it away — too quick. Behind Nefer-Ka, MERYT will not meet his eye.*

### 4. EXT. CAMP — OLAF'S SHADE — DAY
*ASTRID crouches by OLAF. Private.*

**OLAF:** Don't bury me in gold sand, girl. Read us a way back into that seam. Take me home to the grey.

**ASTRID:** *(the reflex)* Tell me it's the right call and I'll do it.

**OLAF:** *(closing his eyes)* That's the one thing I've got left to stop doing for you.

### 5. INT. GUARD TENT — DUSK — SAME DAY
*MERYT and NEFER-KA, low voices. She pours; her hand isn't steady.*

**NEFER-KA:** The seam opened at the suns' touch. That's the alignment. *(watching her)* But it opened *wide*,
and *early*. That isn't the alignment.

**MERYT:** *(too fast)* Omens aren't my duty. The river is.

**NEFER-KA:** No. They're not. *(lets it sit)* Whatever woke it early — it will take a life to put it back to
sleep. I'd like to know whose hand I'm cleaning up after.

*She says nothing. He already half-knows.*

## SEQUENCE 3 — THE FIRST TIDE

### 6. EXT. SHORE — THE NORSE CAMP — NIGHT
*The crew around a driftwood fire that burns wrong-coloured. TOVE sharpens a blade; HELGI turns the wooden horse.*

**TOVE:** You think she'll get us back?

**HELGI:** She can read anything on water. *(beat)* She just won't believe her own eyes without her father's
behind them.

**TOVE:** And if he dies first?

**HELGI:** *(quiet)* Then she learns to look. Hard way.

### 7. EXT. SHORE — FIRST LIGHT OF TWO SUNS — DAWN
*A deep TONE rolls across the desert. The seam flexes. A broad sheet of dune SHUDDERS — and becomes SEA, fast and
deep, racing inland toward the camp. IPY, chasing last night's mirror, is on the wrong side of it.*

**MERYT:** IPY —!

*ASTRID is already moving — she reads the water's run, not the panic — cuts the angle, reaches the child as the
false sea rises to their waists. She throws IPY up the dune to MERYT's arms. The sea-that-was-sand drains; dry
again. ASTRID stands soaked in a desert, shaking.*

**MERYT:** *(clutching Ipy, to Astrid, raw)* You read it. Where it would go. Before it went.

**ASTRID:** *(looking at her own hands, almost afraid)* ...I didn't ask anyone.

### 8. EXT. SHORE — CONTINUOUS
*NEFER-KA has watched the whole thing. He looks from Astrid to the draining sea, recalculating.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(to Meryt, not unkind, final)* It took a child to the edge this time. Next tide it takes the
child. *(beat)* You know what it costs to shut. Tell me, or I guess — and I'm done guessing gently.

### 9. EXT. SHORE — OLAF'S SHADE — NIGHT
*ASTRID sits with OLAF. The adrenaline has left her clear-eyed, frightened by it.*

**ASTRID:** I read the sea without you. And I was right.

**OLAF:** *(the ghost of a smile)* I know. I watched. *(beat)* Took you a whole new world to do it.

**ASTRID:** It doesn't feel like mine yet. The choosing.

**OLAF:** It won't. Right up until it costs you something. Then it's yours for good.

*OFF Astrid — the seam glowing behind her, wider — END ACT I.*

# ACT II — THE BLEED (15:00–42:00)

## SEQUENCE 4 — UNEASY ALLIANCE

### 10. EXT. GUARD CAMP — MORNING OF TWO SUNS
*A tense parley. NORSE on one side, GUARD on the other; ASTRID and NEFER-KA between, MERYT holding the middle.*

**NEFER-KA:** I can read the seam. I cannot be everywhere it tears. *(to Astrid)* You read where the water goes.
Together we keep it from swallowing a town before I can close it.

**ASTRID:** And when it's time to close it?

**NEFER-KA:** That is my burden, not yours. *(beat)* Teach your people the tides. I'll teach you the seam.

**TOVE:** *(low, to Astrid)* We help him shut our only way home?

**ASTRID:** We help him keep a child off the bottom of a sea that shouldn't exist. The rest we argue later.

### 11. EXT. DESERT — SEAM-READING LESSON — DAY
*NEFER-KA traces the seam's light with two fingers; ASTRID mirrors him, fast, hungry. For once she isn't waiting
to be told.*

**NEFER-KA:** It has a grain, like weather. You feel it?

**ASTRID:** *(eyes closed, reading)* It leans. East, then it'll jump — there.
*A far dune ripples to sea exactly where she pointed. NEFER-KA stares — she's learning faster than she should.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(quiet, wary)* ...Who taught you to read like that?

**ASTRID:** Nobody. That was the problem.

## SEQUENCE 5 — THE MARKET

### 12. EXT. RIVER-TOWN MARKET — DAY
*A crowded market at the Nile's edge. IPY shows ASTRID the stalls, proud of her world. HELGI and TOVE trail,
wary, out of place, half-smiling anyway.*

**IPY:** *(Egyptian, subtitled)* My mother sold water here. Before.

**ASTRID:** Before?

**IPY:** Before the morning the suns touched twice.
*ASTRID catches it — "twice." She looks back at MERYT, who heard it too and looks away.*

### 13. EXT. RIVER-TOWN MARKET — THE SECOND TIDE — CONTINUOUS
*The TONE — deeper. ASTRID feels it in her teeth.*

**ASTRID:** *(shouting)* Off the low ground! It comes through the market — NOW!
*People freeze — a foreign woman screaming. MERYT backs her, roaring the order in Egyptian. The crowd scatters as
the market square BECOMES SEA — stalls float, then sink into dune again. Where ASTRID read it, the ground was
clear; where she didn't reach, a cart is lost. No deaths — barely. ASTRID, shaking, has just led a town.*

**MERYT:** *(beside her, awed and ashamed)* They lived because a sea-ghost shouted.

**ASTRID:** They lived because you made them listen. *(beat)* Why did she say *twice*, Meryt?

*MERYT doesn't answer. Yet.*

## SEQUENCE 6 — THE CANYON OF FROZEN WAVES

### 14. EXT. DESERT CANYON — DUSK
*The desync deepens: a canyon where waves of seawater hang FROZEN mid-break, glassy, the light bronze-green. The
crews must cross to reach the seam's root. Beautiful and wrong.*

**NEFER-KA:** The longer it stays open, the less the world remembers which rules it keeps. Walk where I walk.

**HELGI:** *(craning at a frozen wave)* My gran said the dead see the sea stand still. *(to Tove)* Maybe we're
the ghosts here.

### 15. EXT. CANYON — THE ECHO — CONTINUOUS
*A time-echo ripples through: for a moment each person walks beside a translucent DOUBLE of themselves a few
seconds behind. ASTRID's double mouths her father's words. She stops, shaken; NEFER-KA pulls her on.*

**NEFER-KA:** Don't watch the echoes. They show you what you're about to lose, not what is.

### 16. EXT. CANYON FLOOR — NIGHT
*Camp in the frozen canyon. ASTRID and MERYT, apart from the others.*

**ASTRID:** Twice, Meryt. A child said the suns touched *twice* the morning this began. An alignment is once.

**MERYT:** *(finally, quiet)* My guard keeps the old dawn-rite. Light to the suns at the turning. I lit it. *(beat)*
That morning I lit it a second time — for my brother, lost on the river — a thing we are forbidden to do. The
suns touched twice. And the sky tore. *(looks at her hands)* Every ghost that walked out of that sea walked out
of my grief.

**ASTRID:** *(not cruel)* And you let a stranger take the blame of guessing.

**MERYT:** I let a stranger be braver than me. I'm trying to stop.

## SEQUENCE 7 — THE COST

### 17. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — THE THIRD TIDE — DAY
*The seam's root: a towering wound of light. The biggest surge yet floods the approach. HELGI is hauling an anchor
line to steady the crossing when the false sea rises between him and dry ground — fast, chest-high, roaring.*

**ASTRID:** HELGI — the line, drop it, take my hand —

*He's too far. He looks at the wooden horse in his belt — the thing that must get home to a child — and THROWS it
to her, not reaching for her hand.*

**HELGI:** Like he's already gone, Astrid — !

*The sea-that-was-sand pulls him under. Gone. Dry dune where he stood. The wooden horse in ASTRID's fist. TOVE
screams his name into silence.*

### 18. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — AFTER — CONTINUOUS
*ASTRID doesn't collapse. She closes Helgi's horse in her hand and turns to the seam, reading it with a cold new
focus. NEFER-KA watches the change in her.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(gently)* That is the price it takes to stay open. Now you've felt it.

**ASTRID:** *(flat, terrible)* Then it doesn't get another one.

## SEQUENCE 8 — THE TURN

### 19. EXT. GUARD CAMP — NIGHT — STORM OF REFUGEES
*Word has spread: the sea-ghosts' arrival and the tides are linked in the townsfolk's fear. A HIGH OFFICER of the
guard, SETH-NAKHT, arrives with soldiers — above Meryt.*

**SETH-NAKHT:** *(Egyptian, subtitled)* The omens began when the northern ghosts came. Put them in irons. Give
the seam its foreigners and it closes.

**NEFER-KA:** That is not how it closes.

**SETH-NAKHT:** It is how the people sleep tonight.

### 20. EXT. GUARD CAMP — THE BREAK — CONTINUOUS
*Soldiers move on the Norse. TOVE draws; it will be a massacre. MERYT steps between — choosing, at last, out loud.*

**MERYT:** *(to Seth-Nakht, loud, breaking her own secret)* The omens began with ME. My rite. My grief. Not them.
Take the foreigner you want in irons — take me. But they are the only reason this town still has children.

*Stunned silence. SETH-NAKHT hesitates — a confession he didn't expect.*

### 21. EXT. DESERT — ESCAPE UNDER TWO SUNS — NIGHT
*In the confusion ASTRID reads a coming tide and uses it — leads both crews and Ipy across a strip of ground she
knows will flood behind them, cutting off the soldiers without a blow. NEFER-KA, MERYT, the Norse, IPY — one band
now, fused by the same enemy and the same drowning world.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(running beside her)* You turned the thing that's killing us into a wall.

**ASTRID:** It's just reading the water. *(beat, Helgi's horse at her belt)* I'm done not trusting it.

*OFF the seam, now enormous on the horizon, two suns sliding toward each other — END ACT II.*

# ACT III — THE SEAL (42:00–60:00)

## SEQUENCE 9 — CONVERGENCE

### 22. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — THE SUNS CONVERGING — DAY
*The two suns are nearly one. The seam is a vertical wound of light taller than the ship. The desync is constant
now: patches of the desert flicker between sand and sea without warning. The band arrives at the root.*

**NEFER-KA:** When the suns become one, the seam is at its widest — and its weakest. That's when it can be shut.
*(beat)* And only then.

**ASTRID:** How long.

**NEFER-KA:** Less than the time it takes your father to wake and sleep again.

### 23. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — THE PLAN — CONTINUOUS
*ASTRID reads the seam's grain; MERYT organizes the crews; TOVE guards OLAF's litter. NEFER-KA quietly ties back
his robe — preparing himself.*

**MERYT:** *(to Astrid, low)* He means to walk in. He's said his goodbyes to no one, which is how you know.

**ASTRID:** *(watching Nefer-Ka)* He's been ready to die for this since before we fell out of the sky.

**MERYT:** Readiness isn't the same as owed. *(beat)* His people need a guardian who's *alive* when the suns part.

## SEQUENCE 10 — THE CONTESTED SEAL

### 24. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — SUNS BECOMING ONE — DAY
*Blinding convergence. NEFER-KA steps toward the wound of light. ASTRID steps between him and it. She doesn't
argue. She kneels, sets HELGI's wooden horse in the sand, and traces the seam's drift — then points, flat.*

**ASTRID:** There. Next breath.

*The seam PULSES — the exact spot she named ripples to sea and back. She read it to the inch. NEFER-KA stares.*

**NEFER-KA:** *(quietly)* ...You read it. Truly.

**ASTRID:** I never once trusted the water under me. *(beat)* I'm done not trusting it. *(a look at the seam)*
Your world needs its guardian standing on this shore. Not lost in that light.

*Her hand stays on the wooden horse. She doesn't say the rest. NEFER-KA looks from her hand, to the seam she just
read true, and — the hardest thing he's ever done — steps back.*

**NEFER-KA:** Then read it all the way in. Don't flinch at the middle. The middle is where it lies to you.

## SEQUENCE 11 — WHAT THE SEA IS OWED

### 25. EXT. SEAM-ROOT — OLAF'S LITTER — CONTINUOUS
*ASTRID goes to OLAF. He is almost gone. She kneels close.*

**ASTRID:** I can send you home. The ship, Tove, the crew — through the seam before I shut it. The grey sea.
*(breath)* But I can't take you home *and* close it. The seam keeps whoever reads it shut. I can do one.

**OLAF:** *(eyes opening, clear, one last time)* ...The sea takes what it's owed. *(almost a smile)* Owe it me.
Not you. Close it, Astrid. *(beat)* Choose it out loud so I hear you do it once before I go.

**ASTRID:** *(steady, the first unforced choice of her life)* I'm closing the seam. I'm staying. You go home.

*OLAF's hand tightens on hers — proud — and stills. She holds it a moment. She does not weep. Not yet.*

**ASTRID:** *(to Tove, rising)* Take him through. Build him a grey cairn on the gold sand so he knows I was here.
Keep the horse for Helgi's girl.

### 26. EXT. SEAM / SHORE — THE CLOSE — CONTINUOUS
*ASTRID steps into the wound of light. INTERCUT: the ship, the crew, OLAF's body, TOVE clutching the wooden horse
— caught in the seam's pull, then RELEASED backward through it toward grey storm and home. INSIDE the light,
ASTRID reads the seam like a living chart — hands moving — and at the "middle," echoes of her father, of Helgi,
of home crowd her. She does not flinch. She draws the seam CLOSED behind the vanishing ship.*

## SEQUENCE 12 — SINGLE SUN

### 27. EXT. SHORE — THE SUNS PART — DAWN
*The two suns slide apart. One sinks. The false seas drain from the desert for the last time. A single, ordinary
sun rises on an ordinary shore. The seam is gone. ASTRID stands on the sand — this side, alone, alive, exiled
from her own age forever. One choice, and it was the whole of her.*

### 28. EXT. THE CAIRN — SINGLE-SUN MORNING
*A grey-stone cairn on gold sand — wrong and right at once. MERYT sets a marker. IPY places a desert flower. There
is no wooden horse here — it went home with Tove, to a child who needs it. NEFER-KA stands at a respectful
distance, alive, a guardian with a shore to guard.*

**MERYT:** *(beside Astrid)* What do your people call a navigator with no sea to cross?

**ASTRID:** *(a long beat; the first calm of her life)* Home. Eventually. *(looks inland)* I hear this river runs
a long way.

**MERYT:** Longer than anyone's crossed.

**ASTRID:** *(the ghost of Olaf's smile)* Then I'd better read it.

*She turns inland, toward the living world she chose over the one she lost. HOLD: the cairn, the single sun, the
long river. CUT TO BLACK.*

**TITLE: LA PORTE DU SOLEIL.**
