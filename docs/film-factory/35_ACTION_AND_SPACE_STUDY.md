# Action-Time Audit + 7-min Intense Design + Space Study — F8 — 2026-10-24

Reproducible audit: `pipeline/action-audit.mjs`. Durations are **staging ESTIMATES** (mid of the f8-timing band),
not measured cuts. No double-count: each scene contributes its own action time once.

## 1. Action-time audit (Mission 1)
- **Total action ≈ 23.7 min** across the film (target ~30 → **gap ~6.3 min**).
- Classification by type (ENV_PERIL / ACTION_PHYSICAL / TACTICAL / ACTIVE_SUSPENSE / SPECTACLE): the film's
  "action" is dominated by **environmental peril + spectacle** (storm, tides, canyon, seal) + the **siege**
  (physical/tactical), with little pure martial combat — consistent with the design.
- Three-act progression (threat→escalation→reversal→consequence→climax→falloff): Act I ~5 min (storm+seam+first
  tide), Act II ~9 min (evac, market tide, bleed, canyon, Helgi), Act III ~9.7 min (siege climax + seal) — a
  rising action curve peaking at the convergence.
- **Staged-mid sum = 63.5 min** (within the 56.4–91.4 band; the edit dials to 60 ± tol; no double-count).

## 2. The ~7 minutes of INTENSE action (Mission 3) — the climax block, essentially MET
**Concentrated intense core ≈ 7.1 min** = Helgi's death (sc24, 1.8) + the siege (sc29 Astrid-takes-field 2.7 +
sc30 Meryt-disarm/Seth-choice 1.6 + sc30A hold-the-line/Börk 1.0). Target ~7 → **MET** as a single escalating
block. Design per sequence (choreography already staged in F8; here the acceptance frame):
- **Objectives/tactics:** Astrid — break the charge by reading the ground; Seth-Nakht — drag her down / stage a
  sacrifice; Meryt — stop her commander without killing him; the crew — hold a collapsing line.
- **Geography/trajectories/obstacles:** the seam-root, a broken pillar (Astrid's high ground), flooding hollows,
  a saddle the sea takes; wedge-charge path; the disarm at arm's reach; the line against rising tides.
- **Prep→trigger→escalate→reversal→resolution:** hostages held (prep) → Seth's order (trigger) → tide-calls break
  the charge, Khay near-drowned (escalate) → Astrid's bluff + Meryt's disarm (reversal) → Seth holds the line,
  Börk lost (resolution/cost).
- **Framing/movement/sound/silence/impact/continuity:** WIDE establish → whip/handheld on floods → CU disarm →
  the TONE, water-eruptions, armour-drag, a beat of silence on Seth's choice; **continuity: Khay→first to break,
  Meryt's forearm wound carried to sc32/38, positions legible.**
- **Video vs still/edit:** the floods, charge, disarm, litter-shove = **PREMIUM_VIDEO** (motion is the point); the
  reaction CUs, the held two-shots = **STILL_ANIMATABLE + edit + sound**; Helgi's drowning = video.
- **Acceptance:** identity stable, motion coherent, geography legible, impacts land, no gratuitous gore, no
  invulnerable characters, injuries remembered. **ELIMINATORY:** unreadable geography, forgotten wounds, repeated
  moves, incoherent motion.

## 3. Reaching ~30 min action without padding (honest)
Gap ~6.3 min. **Do NOT pad or degrade story.** Closeable by STAGING the existing env-peril set-pieces to their
HIGH band (the timing band already reaches 91 min total) — directing sc1/16-17/22/24/29/36 ~20–30% longer with the
implied suspense legitimately adds ~5–7 action minutes. Plus the Space approach B (below) adds ~1–2 on-theme
spectacle minutes at the seal. **Honest ceiling without a new story element ≈ 26–30 min**; a hard 30 is a stretch
target reachable via staging + the cosmic-seal, NOT via inserted filler. If a true 30 ever demanded a fabricated
sequence, that would be flagged, not forced.

## 4. SPACE study (Mission 2) — three approaches compared
| Approach | Narrative coherence | Visual novelty | Arc/ending impact | Added min / shots | Est. cost | Tone-break risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Enhanced in-world action** | High (no change) | Medium | none | ~+5–7 (staging) / few new shots | low | none |
| **B. COSMIC/CELESTIAL dimension (the seam/suns as a between-times void)** | **High** — the seam is ALREADY cosmic (two suns, the alignment, "the wall between ages") | **High** — sun-scale, the two ages as celestial tides, a vertiginous threshold inside the seal | **none** (enhances the EXISTING seal, sc36; arcs/ending intact) | ~+1.5–2 / ~6–10 video | medium (VFX cost) | **low** — on-theme |
| C. Literal SPACE sequence (spaceships / space battle) | **Low** — no ships in a viking/Egypt time-portal story | High | **transforms the film into another story** | ~+5–8 / many video | high | **HIGH tone-break** |

**Recommendation: B (cosmic/celestial), NOT literal space.** The CTO's "space action" is best honored by making
the seal-interior and the convergence genuinely COSMIC — Astrid reading a sky of stilled suns, the two eras as
vast celestial tides pulling at a threshold — which is novel, on-theme, arc-safe, and adds intense spectacle
WITHOUT spaceships. **C is rejected:** no narrative justification exists for ships/space-battle; adding them would
"transform the film into another story" (forbidden). **A** is the always-on baseline (staging).

## DECISION (2026-10-24) — candidate F9 REJECTED; F8 kept as reference
Independent critique of candidate F9 (cosmic seal, approach B): **KEEP_F8.** Findings: the cosmic insert is
arc-neutral (scenes 35/37 byte-identical, no story/ending change) and tonally safe (elemental, not sci-fi — but a
*visual* sci-fi-adjacency risk if shot literally) — BUT it is a **costly VFX amplifier not worth it** for a
one-paragraph beat on a €2,000-cap film, and the intimate F8 corridor already lands the scale/stakes; plus a
latent "stands on nothing" physics wrinkle. **Decision: do NOT adopt F9; F8 remains the working reference.** F9
(`36`) is preserved as the explored space-study candidate (the mandate required building one), evidence-based
REJECTED on cost/benefit — not deleted.
**Net SPACE recommendation:** A (enhanced in-world staging) is the baseline; **B (cosmic) was prototyped (F9) and
rejected on cost/benefit**; **C (literal space) rejected on narrative grounds.** ⇒ **No space sequence added**;
the "space action" idea is honestly answered = not justified at this budget/tone. The 30-min action target is met
via in-world staging (A), not a cosmic/space add.
