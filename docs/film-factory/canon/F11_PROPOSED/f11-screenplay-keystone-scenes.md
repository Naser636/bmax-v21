## SC01 — EXT. NORTH SEA — STORM — NIGHT (opening hook)

A wall of black water, hill-high. The longship **SŌLFARI** lies beam-on to it — the worst place to be.

**BJÖRN STORMHAND** — a giant, soaked to the bone, the greatsword *Himinkljúfr* lashed across his back — throws
his whole weight on the steering-oar. Beside him, **ASTRID** reads the sea like a page.

> **ASTRID:** She's pulling us side-on! If you hold, she rolls us — if you let go, we lose the next crest!
> **BJÖRN:** *(through his teeth)* Then I hold.
> **ASTRID:** That's not a read, that's pride.
> **BJÖRN:** It's all I've got that she can't take.

The crest curls to break. Where foam should be, the water **UNRAVELS** — a seam of gold light. The sea stops
being the sea. Björn, who has never feared any man, stares into the deep of it — and *that* he fears.

The seam takes the ship.

## SC38 — EXT. SEAM-ROOT — BJÖRN TAKES THE FIELD (Act III battle climax)

Two suns, too close. Seth-Nakht flings a wedge of soldiers at the seam. **ASTRID**, on high stone, reads the ground.

> **ASTRID:** *(calling the inches)* Left of the cracked pillar — THREE — TWO —
> **BJÖRN:** *(to the band)* On her word. Not mine.

He doesn't charge. He **plants** *Himinkljúfr* in the shifting sand and braces — and on her call the ground
**erupts to sea** beneath the wedge. Björn's single, timed strikes turn the ones who get through; no wild swinging,
no joy in it. The sea does the rest.

Seth-Nakht's men look from their commander to the Norseman who makes the water obey a girl's counting — and their
fear changes owner.

> **BJÖRN:** *(not even breathing hard, to Seth-Nakht)* You taught them to be afraid. She taught me what to be
> afraid of. Different lesson.

## SC45 / SC46 — THE CLOSE (the sacrifice — final decisive act is NOT a swing)

The seam hangs open, waiting to be shut. Only a reader who walks in can close it — and does not come back.

Astrid moves toward it. Björn's hand stops her — gently, which from him is strange.

> **BJÖRN:** You read it. I learned to. That's enough for one of us to walk, and only one has nothing left to teach.
> **TOVE:** *(wrecked, trying to joke)* You never could fit through a normal door anyway.
> **BJÖRN:** *(a ghost of a smile)* Tell Börk I said the stew was fine.

He unslings *Himinkljúfr* — and, for the first time, **sets it down**: plants it at the threshold, lets go, and
steps into the light that looks like drowning. The thing he feared most, chosen on purpose.

Inside, he reads it shut. He does not return. The suns part. The sky heals.

*(At the cairn, SC48, his greatsword stands in the stones — a monument to a brute who learned to think, and paid
for it.)*

---

### Why these scenes
- **Voice:** Björn is economical, dry, not stupid — "different lesson." Globally intelligible English, no regional slang.
- **Comedy that serves:** Tove's door joke and the stew callback land *as love* at the hardest moment — relief that deepens grief rather than puncturing it.
- **Arc, not muscle:** his first instinct (hold / cut / charge) fails; his last act is to *set the sword down* — comprehension over force, the whole point.
