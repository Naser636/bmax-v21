**These are strategic targets, NOT acquired revenue, NOT forecasts, NOT guarantees.** This document states the path, the assumptions that must hold, the gaps to close, and the evidence that signals progress. V1's "credible ceiling ~€40–80k / up to €150–300k" was a *current-capability, solo, no-partner* estimate. ## €1M SCENARIO (central target) — recommended Model F (hybrid)
**Composite equation:** €1M ≈ diagnostics €300k (land) + recurring assurance €450k ARR (expand) + partner/subcontract lots €250k (scale).
**12-month shape (illustrative, not a forecast):**
- M1–M3: fix DEF-GAMMA; prove external-repo evidence-pack on 1 consented repo; package OPP-1/2/3 as fixed-fee diagnostics; recruit 1 reviewer + open 1–2 SI partner conversations (future authorization). Revenue ~€0–20k (first diagnostics only if a warm buyer exists).
- M4–M6: 1–2 diagnostics/mo; first recurring retainer(s); first partner-delivered lot. Booked ARR ramping.
- M7–M12: 3–4 diagnostics/mo + 4–8 recurring clients + 2–3 partner lots.
**Prerequisites that MUST be true (else €1M fails):**
1. DEF-GAMMA false-success fixed (precondition to sell assurance honestly). 2. External-repo execution proven + a human-reviewer sign-off workflow. 3. ≥1 external reference engagement (consented, bounded). 4. ≥1 SI/partner delivering under ODG evidence engine. 5. Reviewer + delivery capacity added (ODG cannot sign or staff a program alone). 6. Liability/insurance + claim-wording for "readiness/evidence" (not "compliance/certification").
**Cash & margin:** booked ARR exits Y1 near €1M but **Y1 collected cash is materially lower** (recurring ramps; 30–90-day terms). Gross margin high on ODG-generated evidence; **contributive margin** net of reviewer/partner-share/support/insurance is the real figure — NOT net profit (ignores taxes/overheads/unknowns).
**Confidence:** achievable-but-conditional. If prerequisites 1–4 are met in H1, €1M booked is **plausible (MED)**; €1M *collected* in Y1 is **LOW-MED**. Without them, €1M is not credible.

## €10M SCENARIO (stretch target) — only via product and/or large channel
€10M in the first 12 months is **NOT credible for a solo, reference-less, zero-buyer entity without external capital, a team/partners, and a product**. The arithmetic paths and their hard prerequisites:
- **E1 Product/licence at scale:** €10M ARR = 2,000 repos×€5k or 200 orgs×€50k. Requires a shipped, differentiated product + GTM + support + **funding** (comparable: Snyk >$100M ARR, but a VC-funded company). First credible step: fund + ship a productized evidence/assurance engine with a design partner. Confidence (12mo): **LOW**.
- **E2 OEM/channel embed:** ODG's evidence engine embedded in a large vendor/SI offering under revenue-share; €10M is a slice of their volume. Requires a signed OEM/channel partner + their delivery+support. First step: one embed partnership + integration proof. Confidence (12mo): **LOW / NOT_MEASURABLE**.
- **E3 Enterprise programs via consortium:** 4–10 × €1–3M programs. Requires consortium, references, insurance, certifications, a delivery team, working capital. Not solo-feasible. Confidence (12mo): **LOW**.
**Honest verdict:** €10M/12mo = aspirational; treat as a 2–3-year outcome whose *first credible step* is (product+funding) OR (one large OEM/channel partner). Do NOT present the multi-$B market size as evidence ODG can capture it.

## Human-CTO load & autonomy model (the §7 operational objective)
**ODG does (automatable / already-capable):** opportunity research, source traceability, needs analysis, scoping drafts, pricing models, authorized engineering, SBOM/provenance/parity/readiness evidence generation, tests, independent-ish verification, client-report drafting, recurring re-runs.
**Must stay human / partner (legally or trust-reserved):** signatures & legal commitments; authorization to access client systems; contract validation; **independent technical review + professional sign-off**; professional liability/insurance/compliance; exception/incident ownership; non-automatable sales relationships.
**Minimize CTO daily load by:** (a) the **partner/subcontract model (D)** — the SI owns sales/client/liability; (b) **recurring automation (C)** — onboarding is the only human-heavy step, re-runs are ODG; (c) productized fixed-fee diagnostics — no bespoke scoping each time.
**Autonomy caveat (FACT):** live fleet autonomy = NOT_MEASURABLE (G10 null denominator); autonomous web egress BLOCKED; G8 recovery BLOCKED. So ODG cannot yet be claimed to run delivery unattended — the model assumes ODG-heavy *preparation/engineering/evidence* with human/partner *sales/sign/liability*, not full autonomy.

## Bottom line
€1M (central) is a credible 12-month *target* via Model F **conditional on 6 named prerequisites**, with Y1 *collected* cash likely below €1M. €10M (stretch) needs product+funding and/or a large channel — not solo-feasible in 12 months; its first credible step is a funded product or an OEM/channel partner. All figures are targets/structures, not acquired revenue.
