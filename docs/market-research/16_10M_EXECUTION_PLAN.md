## Accounting interpretation (made explicit, per §3)

## Customer-backward: what justifies €10M of contracts in 12 months
Only a few structures can book €10M in a year; each implies a delivery model ODG must not be the bottleneck for:
- **S1 Anchor OEM/embed/framework deal:** 1 multi-year framework or OEM agreement, €3–5M booked (recognized over term). FACT scale precedent: enterprise framework/IDIQ deals reach $B (Oracle DoW ESI); mid OEM/embed with a large vendor can book multi-€M. Delivery = ODG's evidence engine embedded in the partner's product/service ⇒ partner delivers; ODG supplies software.
- **S2 Enterprise evidence/assurance contracts via partners:** 10–20 × €200–500k (tech-DD at scale, continuous supply-chain/CRA assurance, release-assurance). Delivery = partner/SI pods + ODG evidence engine.
- **S3 Recurring assurance ARR:** 20–40 enterprise retainers × €100–300k ACV (MSSP-style; 65%+ recurring; enterprise ACV $300k–1.2M FACT). Delivery = automated re-runs (best ODG-leverage) + partner onboarding.
- **€10M booked ≈ S1 (€4M) + S2 (12 × €350k = €4.2M) + S3 (15 × €120k = €1.8M).** Every line depends on a **channel** (partners who already own the enterprise relationships) and on ODG being qualified (R2+R4+references+certs+insurance).

## PRIMARY ROUTE — Productized evidence/assurance engine distributed via OEM/channel
**Why primary:** €10M of *delivery* cannot be performed by ODG's labor (solo + reviewer ≈ 2–4 packs/month). The ONLY model where ODG is not the delivery bottleneck is **software that scales without ODG labor** — a packaged "evidence/assurance engine" embedded via OEM/channel + a thin recurring layer. Partners carry sales, delivery, liability, support; ODG supplies the differentiated, reproducible, provenance-bound engine (its one PROVEN asset) at a 20–35% licensor share (FACT).
**What must be TRUE (assumptions):** (a) ≥1 anchor OEM/SI partner adopts+embeds ODG early (M1–M4); (b) ODG packages the engine into an embeddable product (R9-lite); (c) ODG qualifies as a supplier (R2→R4→2 references, certs, insurance); (d) the €100k envelope (+ likely further funding) funds packaging + first reviewer/BD hires + certs/insurance; (e) the anchor partner has ≥€30–50M relevant end-customer volume to yield ODG's share.
**Honest confidence:** booking €10M in Y1 on this route is **LOW and assumption-heavy** — it requires an anchor partner to commit and ramp within the year. It is the *strongest* route *because software delivery scales*, not because it is likely without the anchor.

## CONTINGENCY ROUTE — Direct high-value diagnostics → remediation + recurring (no anchor partner)
If no anchor partner materializes: direct productized diagnostics (OPP-1/2/3) + remediation lots + recurring retainers. **Realistic Y1 signed ceiling ≈ €1–3M** (delivery-capacity-bound even with a few reviewer pods) — **explicitly below €10M**. Per §1 this is a sensitivity/fallback, not a replacement for the primary target; it is the honest floor if the channel assumption fails.
