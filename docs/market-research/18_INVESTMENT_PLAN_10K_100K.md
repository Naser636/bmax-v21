# Staged Investment Plan — €10k initial / €100k potential (Study V4, 2026-10-22)

**No spend is authorized or performed by this mission (§14).** This is a milestone-gated PLAN. Spend only on explicit CTO approval. Prefer €0 options where capability already exists. Distinguish: revenue ≠ gross margin ≠ contribution margin ≠ operating profit ≠ cash.

## Key €0 insight — the highest-value unlock may cost nothing
**R2 on a PUBLIC open-source repository = €0 and real.** Analyzing a *public* OSS project's public lockfile/licences (SBOM, provenance, licence-risk, reproducibility) is analysis of public data — it proves external-repo execution on **real, non-synthetic** code without accessing anyone's *private* repo and without payment. (It is NOT a paying-client validation — that still needs a client + R4.) This should be the first move; it de-risks the entire strategy for €0. *(Distinct from the mandate's bar on private third-party repos.)*

## Working capital, margin & cash (modelled)
- **Deliver-before-collect gap:** diagnostics/lots bill on milestones (30–90d); recurring ramps; partner-delivered work may pay on partner terms. Y1 **collected cash < booked**.
- **Margins:** high gross margin on ODG-generated evidence; **contribution margin** net of reviewer/partner-share (20–40%)/support/insurance is the real figure; **operating profit** further nets BD/overhead/taxes. None of the €10M booked figure is profit.
- **Single-channel dependency:** the primary route concentrates risk on one anchor partner → T3 funds a second partner to diversify.
