# 12-Month Financial Model — $1M and $10M arithmetic (Study V3, 2026-10-22)

Extends V2 `05`. **All figures are assumptions/structures to evaluate, NOT forecasts, NOT acquired revenue.** Conversion = NOT_MEASURABLE (no sales history) — scenarios, not probabilities. Price anchors are FACT-sourced (V1 `03`, V2 `07`, V3 `12`). Separate throughout: TAM → documented comparable spend → accessible opportunity → publicly-identified-unqualified prospects → qualified buyers (**0**) → proposals (**0**) → signed (**0**) → invoiced (**$0**) → collected (**$0**).

## Capacity & cost assumptions (HYP, explicit)
- ODG+1 reviewer ≈ **2–4 small diagnostics/month**; a €100k lot ≈ 15–30 delivery-days. Solo ODG cannot staff concurrent large programs ⇒ scale needs partners/pods.
- Costs: reviewer (human day-rate), partner share (SI 20–40% / OEM licensor keeps 20–35%), compute/provider, insurance, support. **Gross margin high on ODG-generated evidence; contribution margin is net of these; neither is net profit** (ignores taxes/overheads/unknowns).
- Cash: diagnostics 30-day terms; recurring ramps (a client signed in month M yields ~ (12−M) months of Y1 cash); lots milestone-billed 30–90 days ⇒ **Y1 collected < Y1 booked**.

## $1M Year-1 (Model F hybrid) — monthly shape (illustrative)
Equation: **$1M ≈ diagnostics €300k + recurring $450k ARR + partner lots €250k.**
| Qtr | Diagnostics | Recurring (new ARR) | Partner lots | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | €0–20k | €0 | €0 | R1 done; R2 proof; package offers; recruit reviewer; (future) partner convos |
| Q2 | €40–80k | €60–120k | €0–60k | first references; first retainer(s); first subcontract lot |
| Q3 | €80–120k | €120–180k | €80–120k | 2–4 diag/mo; 3–5 retainers; 1–2 lots |

**Booked ≈ $1M exiting Y1; collected materially lower.** Confidence: booked MED (if 6 prerequisites met H1), collected LOW-MED.

## $10M Year-1 — arithmetic per route + feasibility
| Route | $10M equation | Units needed | Operational feasibility Y1 | Working capital / funding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A enterprise direct | 5 × $2M programs | ~5 mega-programs | **NO** (no refs/team/insurance/consortium) | high (deliver-before-collect) |
| B SI subcontract | 10,000 days × €1,000 | ~50 delivery pods | **NO solo**; only as a component of SI framework volume | medium (via partner terms) |
| C OEM/embed | 20–35% × ~$28–50M partner volume | 1 large partner at scale | **NO in Y1** (sign+integrate+ramp); plausible Y2–3 | low for ODG (partner funds GTM) |
| D recurring | 40 × $250k or 200 × $50k ACV | 40–200 enterprise clients | **NO solo** (onboarding capacity) | medium (ramp) |
| E diag→remediation | 400 diag + conversions | 400+ engagements | **NO solo** | low-medium |
| F product/licence | 200 × $50k ARR | shipped product + GTM | **NO in Y1** (build+funding) | **high (funding required)** |
| G hybrid | blend | multiple channels | **NO in Y1 without a pre-existing large channel + funding** | high |
**Conclusion:** no route reaches $10M in Year-1 under ODG's real capacity without (a) a pre-existing large OEM/SI channel adopting ODG at scale, and/or (b) funding + product + team. **$10M/Y1 = NOT feasible as modelled; feasible as a Y2–3 channel outcome.** First cash/funding need for the credible scale path (C): partner-integration engineering + 1–2 reviewer hires ≈ modest; the *big* funding need is Route F (product), deferred.

## Single-customer / single-channel dependency & failure scenarios
- The scale routes (C especially) create **high single-channel dependency** on one partner. Mitigation: diversify to ≥2 partners before treating channel revenue as durable.
- Failure scenarios: no partner signs (→ fall back to direct diagnostics E/B, ceiling ~$1M); reference engagement fails (→ no program entry; fix engine, retry); recurring churn (→ ARR erodes). Each maps to a falsifiable gate in `08`.

## Sensitivity (what moves the outcome)
- **Dominant levers:** (1) does R2 external-repo proof + R4 reference happen (gates B/C/D/E); (2) does ≥1 partner sign (gates C/B scale); (3) reviewer/pod capacity (gates delivery volume). Remove (1) → credible Y1 ceiling collapses to V2's ~€40–150k (direct diagnostics only). Remove (2) → $10M off the table entirely.
- Price ±30%, conversion unknown (scenarios not %), collection delay 30–90d, partner share 20–40% — all modeled as ranges, none as a committed number.

## Bottom line
**$1M Year-1 = credible target (Model F, conditional). $10M Year-1 = NOT feasible as modelled** (needs pre-existing large channel + funding); it is the Year-2/3 objective of a proven channel. Nothing here is signed, invoiced, or collected.
