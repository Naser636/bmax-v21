# Selection Rationale — ODG_ORIGINAL_FILM_DEVELOPMENT_V1

**Selected concept: #1 — "QUIET CAR"** (contained thriller / moral drama, short film ~22 min).

## Why this one (comparative justification against the rubric)
1. **Dramatic causality & escalation (decisive):** a single confined engine — one night, one corridor of
   track, a terminus as a hard deadline — forces every scene to raise the stakes by cause and effect. There is
   no room for filler; the clock does structural work the other concepts would need subplots to achieve.
2. **Dialogue (decisive for a *text* demonstration):** a two-hander lives or dies on spoken subtext. This is
   the hardest and most revealing thing to write well, and it is exactly what the CTO asked to see proven — not
   narration. Concept 4 also favours dialogue but diffuses it across an episodic road; Concept 1 concentrates
   it.
3. **A fair, earned reversal + satisfying denouement:** the premise contains a *moral* inversion (the hunter is
   the hunted; the seeker of a confession must give one) that can be fully *planted* and paid off within the
   runtime — the "ending" dimension the brief weights heavily. It resolves emotionally, not mechanically.
4. **Character under pressure:** confinement externalises interior conflict. Maren needs the man to be guilty
   to keep her grief coherent; that need — not a villain — is the antagonist. Contradictory objectives are
   built into the premise.
5. **Feasibility as a bounded, affordable text dossier:** two principal characters, one location, no VFX, no
   choreography. It can be developed to real depth here, and (later, separately) produced cheaply — aligning
   with the honest economics in `07_MISSION_CINEMA_STUDY.md`.

## Why not the others (honest)
- **#2 Understudy's Mother** — strongest *visual* idea (dance, mirrors, decay) but that power lives off the
  page; a text dossier would under-serve its best asset, and dementia-as-structure risks confusion if mishandled.
- **#3 Cartographer of Debts** — the most *original* premise, and a real contender; set aside because
  world-building would crowd out character/dialogue in a bounded deliverable, making it the weakest vehicle to
  *prove* the specific craft the brief asks for. **Flagged as the top candidate for a future, more ambitious
  development mission** where its originality is the point.
- **#4 Low Tide Radio** — warm and castable, but the most familiar shape (road + reconciliation) and prone to
  episodic sag; lower originality ceiling.

## What selection does and does not claim
- **Claims:** of the four, #1 has the best *potential* to demonstrate credible dialogue, tight causality, a fair
  reversal, and a satisfying ending in a text dossier.
- **Does NOT claim:** that the resulting dossier is artistically good. That is the human reviewer's call
  (`human_review` in the contract); ODG will only verify internal consistency.
