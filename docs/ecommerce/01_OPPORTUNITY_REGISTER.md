## LEAD A — Authentic branded apparel / sneakers (grey-market wholesale) — **DISCARDED (authenticity risk)**
- Customer/need (FACT): strong resale demand; Vinted charges sellers **0%** in France (buyers pay 5%+€0.70) →
  attractive channel economics.
- Sourcing/authenticity (FACT — decisive): grey-market wholesalers (e.g., Griffati) **have documented
  counterfeit reports** — a buyer received a Tommy Hilfiger counterfeit warning letter; suppliers claim invoices
  but reports undermine it. Per mandate §4, discard suppliers whose authenticity/resale-rights can't be
  reasonably established. **Counterfeit resale is a civil + criminal offence in France.**
- Verdict: **DISCARDED.** The margin is real but the legal/authenticity risk on a €10k budget is unacceptable
  and cannot be de-risked without an *authorized official distributor* contract (not available here).

## LEAD B — Premium indoor-cat enrichment & accessories — **RECOMMENDED (see `02`)**
- Customer/need (FACT): France ~77M pets (cats > dogs); FR pet-products market **$2.3B (2026) → $2.8B (2031)**;
  "pet humanization"/premiumisation; cats CAGR **9.09%** driven by urban indoor living + behavioural enrichment.
- Demand/trend (FACT): premium feeding accessories, enrichment, bedding, carriers, home-integrated accessories.
- Competition/price (NON_VERIFIED): category ASP €20–24 EST from browsing; **SKU-level prices + Amazon-FR
  bestseller ranks NOT pulled** → NON_VERIFIED.
- Seasonality: low (year-round; gifting uplift Q4). Dead-stock risk: moderate (durable, non-perishable, non-sized
  → low return rate vs apparel).
- Sourcing ease (FACT): verifiable EU wholesalers (Ankorstore FR/EU, BigBuy ES) — see `03`.
- Margin after all costs (EST/model): CM/unit €0.44 (prudent) → €4.23 (favorable) — THIN; see `05`.
- Capital/rotation: light parcels, fast-ish rotation; €3.2–4.8k initial stock keeps a reserve.
- Logistics/returns/CS: small parcels (Colissimo/Mondial Relay); low sizing-return risk; CS manageable.
- Legal: GPSR product-safety info; pet accessories generally non-regulated (NOT food/medicine — avoid pet-food
  which triggers feed law). Resale rights clean (generic/own-brand-able).
- Growth: own-brand potential; adjacent cat/dog expansion. Major risk: THIN margins + unverified unit economics.
