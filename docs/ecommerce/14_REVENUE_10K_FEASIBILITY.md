# €10,000 revenue in Month 1 — feasibility verdict (ODG_ECOMMERCE_OPERATOR_V1)

**Computed** (sandbox; inputs EST/NON_VERIFIED). The target is treated as an objective to test, not a result.
Revenue ≠ profit ≠ cash ≠ owner pay.

## Required funnel to reach €10,000 revenue
- AOV (EST) **€32** → **≈313 orders** needed in month 1.
- Conversion + traffic + ad economics differ sharply by channel:

| Channel | Conv. rate | Sessions needed | Ad spend to get them | CAC | Contribution/order (before ad) | Net/order after ad | Verdict |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| **Own cold shop** | 1.8% | **17,389** | **€9,564** (CPC €0.55) | €30.56 | €12.17 | **−€18.39** | **NOT viable** — ad cost > contribution; burns 96% of capital at a loss |
| **Marketplace (Amazon.fr)** | 8% (product sessions) | ~3,913 | ~€1,500 (modest sponsored) | ~€5 | €10.46 | ~€5 | **conditionally feasible as a TOP-RANGE outcome, NOT central, NOT guaranteed** |

## Verdict (honest, per §3 — ambition NOT reduced arbitrarily)
**€10,000 revenue in month 1 is NOT sufficiently supported for an independent cold storefront** within a €10k
capital budget: acquiring ~17k cold sessions costs ~€9.5k in ads (96% of capital) and loses ~€18/order — the
model collapses. It is **conditionally feasible ONLY via a marketplace** (existing demand), and even there it is
a **favorable/top-range** outcome for a brand-new seller (ranking + review velocity take time), not the central
case. The prior model's central case was ~€6k revenue/month — so €10k is the optimistic edge, not the base.

## Required MODEL CHANGE to make €10k credible (not a smaller ambition — a different lever)
1. **Lead with the marketplace** (Amazon.fr, later + Cdiscount) for *volume* — it supplies the traffic a cold
   shop cannot afford. The own-shop is for *margin/brand*, added after a SKU is proven (not the month-1 engine).
2. **Raise AOV** (bundles, free-ship threshold €49, higher-ticket hero SKU) to cut the orders-needed from 313.
3. **Validate the two decisive unknowns first** (supplier quote → real unit cost; marketplace pull → real ASP +
   rank/velocity). Without them, €10k is a hypothesis, not a plan.
4. Realistic month-1 expectation for a new seller is **€2–6k revenue** (central), with €10k reachable only if
   demand + ranking break favorably. Chasing €10k via paid traffic on an own-shop would **destroy the capital**.

## What would change this verdict (real observations)
Actual marketplace conversion + session volume for the chosen SKUs; a hero SKU with higher AOV + strong early
reviews; a supplier cost low enough to fund sponsored ads profitably. Until observed, treat €10k month-1 as the
ambitious ceiling, with **€2–6k the honest central expectation**.
