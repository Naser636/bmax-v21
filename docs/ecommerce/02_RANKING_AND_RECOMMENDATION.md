## Scoring method (explicit, evidence-weighted)
Each lead scored 1–5 on: **Demand** (sourced market signal), **Margin** (contributive, after all costs),
**Rotation** (speed to cash), **Risk↓** (legal/authenticity/dead-stock, higher=safer), **Simplicity** (logistics/
returns/CS), **Growth**, **Verifiability** (how sourced the unit economics are). Weights reflect the €10k,
low-capital, first-activity context: Risk↓ ×2 and Margin ×2 (a thin-margin or legally-risky activity fails
fastest at this capital), Demand ×1.5, others ×1. Scores are the provider's evidence-based judgement; they are
NOT a profit guarantee.

## Recommendation: **LEAD B — Premium indoor-cat enrichment & accessories**
**Why it wins (from evidence, not spectacle):**
1. **Risk↓ (decisive at €10k):** generic/own-brandable → no counterfeit/resale-rights problem (unlike A, which
   is DISCARDED on documented counterfeit reports); non-food → no feed-law burden; durable → low dead-stock.
2. **Durable, growing, sourced demand:** FR pet market $2.3B→$2.8B; cats CAGR 9.09%; humanization is structural,
   not a fad — lower fad-risk than a single "winning product."
3. **Operational simplicity:** small, light, non-sized parcels → low return rate and cheap shipping (vs bulky
   home-org C, heavier ergonomic E, authenticity-fraught A).
4. **Growth path:** own-brand + adjacent cat/dog lines once a hero SKU is validated.

**Why not the others:** A discarded (counterfeit/legal); C/E close runners-up but bulk/competition hurt margin
and simplicity; D saturated; F too fragmented and least verifiable.

## What this recommendation does NOT claim
- It does **not** claim the activity is profitable. The model (`05`) shows the **prudent scenario LOSES ~€356
  over 3 months**; margins are thin and depend entirely on **NON_VERIFIED** unit cost (€8 EST) and ASP (€20–24
  EST).
- The decisive next validations before any capital: (1) **≥2 supplier quotes** (real wholesale cost + MOQ +
  shipping) and (2) a **marketplace demand pull** (Amazon-FR/Cdiscount actual sold prices + best-seller ranks
  for 5–10 candidate SKUs). Until both exist, the recommendation is **evidence-led but not profit-proven.**
