# Advertising & Acquisition Plan (prepared, NOT launched) — ODG_ECOMMERCE_OPERATOR_V1

Prepared assets only. **No ad account, no campaign, no spend** (mandate §6). Budgets are caps to test, not
commitments. All performance figures are EST until a real, observed test runs.

## Channel priority (from `14` feasibility)
1. **Amazon.fr Sponsored Products** (primary) — advertise *into existing demand*; pay-per-click on bottom-funnel
   keywords; no cold-traffic gamble. Budget cap €150–300/mo (scenario-dependent). Stop rule: ACOS > 35%.
2. **Meta (Instagram/Facebook) retargeting only** (secondary, later) — retarget shop visitors, NOT cold
   prospecting (cold Meta prospecting to a new shop is the money-losing path in `14`). Budget cap €100/mo.
3. **Organic**: optimised Amazon listings + basic SEO on the own-shop; the real margin lever.

## Amazon Sponsored — prepared structure
- **Campaigns:** (a) Auto-discovery (harvest search terms, 2 wks) → (b) Manual exact on winning terms → (c)
  Product-targeting on competitor ASINs. Budget split 40/40/20.
- **Keywords (EST, to validate):** "arbre à chat", "fontaine à eau chat", "griffoir chat", "gamelle anti
  glouton", "tunnel chat". Negatives harvested weekly.
- **Bid discipline:** start at suggested bid × 0.8; raise only on terms with orders; pause terms with spend >
  2× target CPA and 0 orders.

## Offers / creatives (prepared text, original — no brand impersonation)
- Hero angle: "Le confort malin pour chats d'intérieur." Free shipping ≥ €49 (drives AOV).
- Listing bullets: GPSR-compliant (identity + safety), benefit-led, size/material specifics, FR language.
- Images: 6 SKUs × (main on white + lifestyle + size/scale) — **to be produced** (human/photo; not available here).

## Landing pages
- Marketplace: the product listing is the LP.
- Own-shop: `shop/index.html` product grid + product detail (prototype built) → optimise later for a paid LP.

## KPIs + stop rules (see also `10`)
ACOS ≤ 20% target / stop > 35%; CTR, CVR, CPC, CPA vs contribution/order (€10.46 marketplace — **ad CPA must stay
below it**); daily budget caps; weekly review. **Hard rule from `14`:** never buy cold traffic whose CPA exceeds
contribution/order — that is the capital-destroying path.

## Blocked / needs authorization before launch
Ad-account creation, billing method, pixel/tag install, and any spend = **human + authorization** (Phase-2).
Prepared here: structure, keywords, negatives, bid rules, budget caps, creative text, KPI + stop rules.
