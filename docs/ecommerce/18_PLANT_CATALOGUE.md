## The decisive catalogue finding (honest)
**Cheap, high-demand accessories (PL-01/02/06) LOSE money sold singly** once a ~€4.50 shipping cost + 15% fee +
12.3% cotisations hit a ~€13 price. They are **traffic drivers and bundle fillers**, NOT standalone profit. The
profit engine is the **higher-ticket anchors** (stand €4.87, grow light €3.78, planter €2.42) and **bundles that
clear the €49 free-shipping threshold**.

**Pricing rule derived:** bundles must either clear €49 (free-ship, margin-positive) or carry buyer-paid
shipping; a sub-€49 bundle of only cheap SKUs is a loss. The "Kit Débutant" must gain an anchor or buyer-paid
shipping to be viable — a concrete pre-launch fix.

## Why a new seller can compete here
Not on price vs commodity giants, but on **curation + bundles + content** (organic plant-care social demand),
**low-return durable items**, and **repeat** (plant food subscription). Differentiation = a coherent "new-plant
starter system," not a single me-too SKU.

## Caveats
Every costEst is NON_VERIFIED (quote decides). Ceramic pots carry **breakage/return risk** in transit (confirm
packaging + carrier with supplier). ASPs are observed snapshots, not guaranteed sell prices for a new listing.
