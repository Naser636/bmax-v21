## Chosen launch channel (justified by data): **Amazon.fr, self-ship (FBM) first**
- **Why Amazon.fr primary:** it supplies *existing demand* — the single biggest risk at €10k is "built a shop,
  no traffic." A marketplace removes the paid-acquisition gamble the mandate explicitly warns against (§5: do
  not assume a shop or an ad campaign generates sales). Cost is ~15% + €39/mo — high, but paid only on sales.
- **Why FBM (self-ship), NOT FBA, initially:** FBA adds fulfilment fees + locks inventory + ties up cash; FBM
  keeps cash and stock under control while demand is unproven. Revisit FBA only after a validated hero SKU.
- **Secondary:** Cdiscount (added in month 2–3 for incremental FR reach). **Own Shopify = later** (only once a
  SKU is proven on marketplaces; otherwise its traffic cost is a money pit at this budget).
- **Vinted excluded** for Lead B despite 0% fees: it is a second-hand/fashion channel, not a fit for new niche
  pet accessories (it WOULD be the channel if Lead A/apparel had survived — but A is discarded).

## Acquisition strategy (realistic) + tracking
- Organic: optimised Amazon listings (GPSR-compliant, keyworded), 8–10 SKUs, strong photos.
- Paid: a *small* Sponsored-Products budget only (modelled €50–300/mo by scenario) — NOT a growth assumption;
  sales are driven by marketplace demand + listing quality, not by assumed ad ROAS.
- KPIs (see `10`): sessions→unit conversion, ACOS (ad cost/sales), sell-through, return rate, contribution/unit.
- **Honest caveat:** a listing live ≠ sales. Until real sessions/conversion are observed, projected unit sales
  are EST only.
