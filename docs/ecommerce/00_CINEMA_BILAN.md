## Capabilities DEMONSTRATED
- ODG can **orchestrate** a creative mission end-to-end: contract → scope → acceptance criteria → human-review
  gate → independent verification → compass bookkeeping.
- ODG can run **deterministic structural/consistency verification** over prose: provenance (sha256/sourceCommit
  on factual claims), character-roster consistency, continuity/name checks, setup→payoff tracking, dialogue-vs-
  narration ratio, section/format completeness, banned-term scans. These caught **real defects** (a non-byte-
  faithful quote; untagged setups) that were then corrected — genuine value.

## Capabilities NOT demonstrated
- **No native creative generation.** Concepts, prose, and dialogue were authored by the bounded provider.
- **No audiovisual capability** (voice/image/video/music/edit) — never in scope, still absent.
- **No artistic-quality judgement.** ODG cannot assess merit; that is human-only.
- **No validated artistic success.** The film's mandatory human artistic verdict was never issued → "Quiet Car"
  is NOT a validated work; only structural checks (24/24 consistency) passed, which is explicitly not merit.
- **No true-originality proof** (offline; no comparison to existing works).

## Real role of the Claude delegation
- The creative writing was **100% bounded provider (Claude) delegation**, declared before use and accounted as
  delegation. It must **never** be counted as ODG-native autonomy. ODG's native share = orchestration +
  verification + bookkeeping.

## Limits of the structural checks
- A structural PASS proves **internal consistency/form only** — not quality, not audience appeal, not demand,
  not legal originality. "Process success ≠ business/artistic success."

## Reusable learnings → carried into the e-commerce mission
1. **Declare every delegation up front** (need, scope, write-set, cost, acceptance, expected evidence) and
   verify independently — the exact governance the e-commerce mission must apply to supplier/price research.
2. **Deterministic verification over provider output works** — the same pattern applies to the financial model
   (recompute, no double-count) and to supplier claims (VERIFIED vs SPOTTED).
3. **Separate proven-by-structure from unknown-until-reality** — mirrors "model PASS ≠ real demand/profit".
4. **Mark NON_VERIFIED honestly** rather than inventing — directly reused for prices/stock/quotes.
5. **A human gate for the irreducible judgement** — artistic verdict there; go/no-go on real capital spend here.
