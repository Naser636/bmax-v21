Compliance items are FACT-sourced where cited (2026-10-23, `12_SOURCES.md`); depth tagged NON_VERIFIED where a

## Compliance obligations (France / EU, apply to the recommended Lead B)
1. **GPSR — Reg. (EU) 2023/988, in force since 2024-12-13 (FACT):** applies to non-food consumer products w/o
   a sector-specific regime. Each listing must carry manufacturer/importer identity + safety info/warnings;
   traceability. Pet accessories are in scope.
2. **Distance-selling pre-contract information (FACT):** before sale, display legibly — seller identity +
   contact, essential product characteristics, **total price TTC incl. delivery**, delivery time, **14-day
   right of withdrawal + standard form**, legal guarantees (conformity + hidden defects), consumer mediator,
   legal mentions (LCEN), and GDPR/RGPD information. Requires CGV + mentions légales pages.
3. **Micro-entreprise fiscal/social (FACT + EST):** VAT **franchise-en-base** (goods <€85k) → no VAT invoiced,
   **no input-VAT recovery**; must show "TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI". Social cotisations ≈ **12.3%**
   of revenue for sale-of-goods (EST; 2026 exact NON_VERIFIED); optional *versement libératoire* income-tax
   (~1% of goods revenue) if eligible (NON_VERIFIED). Registration via the guichet unique (free).
4. **REP / Triman / packaging eco-contribution (NON_VERIFIED depth):** France extended-producer-responsibility
   + Triman sorting logo likely apply to packaging and some products; eco-contribution registration (e.g.,
   CITEO) may be required. **Flagged — must be confirmed with a specialist before listing.**
5. **Product liability + conformity:** seller liable for conformity; keep supplier invoices + GPSR docs.

## Business risks (ranked)

|---|---|---|
| **Unverified unit economics** (cost €8 / ASP €20–24 EST) | **High** | get ≥2 supplier quotes + marketplace price pull BEFORE any buy; model recomputes on real inputs |
| **Thin margins** (prudent CM/unit €0.44 → loss) | **High** | require ASP−cost spread validated; drop SKUs below a CM/unit floor; keep fixed low (FBM, no Shopify yet) |
| **No demand / listing ≠ sales** | **High** | launch on a marketplace with existing traffic (Amazon.fr), not an owned shop; validate with a small SKU set first |
| **Dead stock** | Medium | small first order (400–600u), demand-triggered reorders, durable non-perishable items |
| **Amazon account suspension / policy** | Medium | compliant listings, genuine invoices, strong CS metrics; Cdiscount as backup channel |
| **Cash-flow (payout delay)** | Medium | €4,600 reserve; reorder from reserve not from uncollected sales |
| **Counterfeit/IP** | **High (if branded)** → eliminated | Lead A discarded; stick to generic/own-brandable SKUs |
| **Returns/refunds** | Medium | non-sized items; clear listings/photos; model carries 3–8% |
| **Supplier reliability / MOQ surprise** | Medium | two VERIFIED suppliers (Ankorstore Net-60, BigBuy no-MOQ); confirm MOQ in quote |
| **Compliance miss (GPSR/REP)** | Medium-High | legal/accountant gate before Phase-2; GPSR fields on every listing |

## Hard stop / escalation triggers
- Supplier quote makes central CM/unit < €1.50 → **do not proceed** with that SKU/supplier.
- Marketplace pull shows ASP materially below €20 for viable SKUs → re-select niche.
- Compliance (REP/GPSR) cannot be met within the admin envelope → pause for specialist review.
