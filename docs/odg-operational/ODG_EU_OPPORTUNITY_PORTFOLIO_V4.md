- **NONE of the €100k–€500k+ programs is end-to-end ODG-deliverable TODAY.** They require accredited consultants,
  legal/liability cover, insurance, human experts (offensive security / auditors), and in FinOps a new ODG
  capability (cloud rightsizing). ODG's genuinely-deliverable slice at each is the **automation + evidence +
  documentation sub-component** (lower contract value but real margin + recurring), OR a **partner model** (ODG as
  the automation engine behind a human-fronted consultancy/MSSP).

  wave** — mandatory dated demand + ODG's real evidence/audit/SBOM/license capabilities + recurring (continuous
  obligation) + a partner/white-label channel lowers the accreditation/liability barrier. This bridges C1 (audit)
  and C4 (governance/evidence).

  €300K-1.2M/yr) but **NOT immediately executable** — requires a partner + accreditation + capital. Retained as
  strategic, not declared executable (§6).
