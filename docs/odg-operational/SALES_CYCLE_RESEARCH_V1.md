## Ranked — shortest time-to-first-cash (brand-new solo, no references)
1. **Paid pilot (#2)** — smallest ask, discretionary budget, references least decisive; a 2-week POC compresses months of evaluation. **Highest-leverage entry.**
2. **One-off audit (#1)** — discrete, single PO (21–45 days); independence sometimes preferred over incumbency.
3. **Partner sell-through (#6)** — fast per deal, but must first win the partner (second cycle).
4. **SBOM/CRA pack (#3)** — real tailwind but binding deadline is Dec-2027 → soft urgency now.
5. **Post-audit remediation (#4)** — near-certain to close but only AFTER an audit (second-order).
6. **Recurring subscription (#5)** — best outcome, slowest first close for an unknown.

## Land-and-expand (pattern evidence, NOT demand)
Canonical motion: **paid wedge → proven result → expand.** Paid pilots bought by the **budget holder** convert ~**60–90%**
to the larger deal; ~**6%** when the signer lacks production budget; ~**12%** enterprise AI POC→prod (arcshift, 1,500+
engagements). Decisive variable = **who signs the pilot**, not pilot quality. A successful paid pilot (#2) → remediation
(#4) → monitoring retainer (#5), chained in one account.

## Conclusion (honest)
**Lead with a tightly-scoped, budget-holder-funded paid PILOT (or one-off audit), designed so remediation + a monitoring
retainer are the obvious next steps.** This is a structural/pattern inference — **not validated demand**; no prospect,
trigger instance, or intent was verified for ODG.
