## Net definition (layers, kept strictly distinct)
gross cash collected → **−unpaid/refunds** = collected → **−direct costs** (acquisition+delivery+subcontract+commission)
= operating net → **−social charges** (shown separately) → **−income tax** (shown separately, flagged) = **NET AVAILABLE.**
No double-count; annual subscription paid monthly counts only the months collected; partner commission + subcontract deducted.

## What €500k NET requires (inverse) — the honest, sobering result
- **Solo:** NET €500k needs **~€1,492,983 GROSS cash COLLECTED** in-period (**≈3.0× the net target**). Signed/invoiced
  are higher still (lag).
- **Heavy hybrid** (subcontract 30% + commission 15% + own delivery/acquisition + social): **NOT ACHIEVABLE** — direct
  costs + 25.6% social on turnover exceed gross ⇒ **net goes negative.** The channel economics that reached €500k GROSS

## Verdict vs modeled capacity (no false success)
Year-1 **cash** modeled best-case ≈ **€342k–448k** (V9 monthly / V16 gap-closure signed trajectory). €500k **NET** needs
**~€1.49M gross collected** — roughly **3–4× beyond** any modeled year-1 cash. ⇒ **€500k NET cash in the first 12 months
is NOT achievable under any modeled scenario (solo or hybrid).** It is a **multi-year** trajectory, or requires a
fundamentally different (higher-margin, higher-volume, team-based) structure. Stated plainly, not disguised.

## Corrective levers (to shrink the gap — each HYPOTHESIS, measure before trusting)
1. **Maximise net factor:** high-margin DIRECT work (avoid stacking commission+subcontract which zero out net); keep
   delivery cost low (automation/reproducible kits — assurance-pilot-runner); minimise bad-debt (deposits/milestones).
2. **Raise gross collected:** high contract value + recurring + the paid-pilot→retainer chain; more qualified volume.
3. **Structure:** above the micro ceiling (€83,600) a **SASU** changes the social/tax profile (IS vs micro-social on
   turnover) — can materially change the net factor; **expert-comptable decision** (not modeled as advice).
4. **Timeline:** treat €500k NET as a **≥18–24-month** objective; set an honest, lower year-1 NET milestone + a run-rate
   exit, explicitly as a milestone, not as the €500k target met.
