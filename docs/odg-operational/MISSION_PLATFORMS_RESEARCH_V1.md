## Public tenders (reconfirmed)
- **< €40 000 HT = gré à gré** — buyer may contract **without publicity/competition** = lowest-barrier public entry
  (via an existing relationship). (economie.gouv.fr DAJ / service-public.)
- €40k → EU thresholds = **MAPA** (buyer sets rules). 2026 formalized thresholds ~€140k (État) / €216k (collectivités) —
  **re-verify on the official DAJ seuils page.** Portals: PLACE, APProch, regional, BOAMP.
- **Verdict (reconfirmed):** solo legally eligible but **practically disadvantaged** (references/financials/insurance/
  effort); realistic solo entry = **< €40k gré à gré via a relationship**, not open MAPA. Time-to-first = months.

## SBOM/CRA marketplace — honest finding
**No freelance marketplace specifically distributes SBOM/CRA assurance missions to independents.** Bug-bounty platforms
(YesWeHack/Yogosha/Intigriti) pay for finding vulnerabilities (different skill). SBOM/CRA tooling vendors (Anchore,
Cybeats, sbomify) are tools/listings, not mission channels. ⇒ **the SBOM/assurance offer must ride generalist platforms
(Malt), direct outreach, or partner collectives (Voie D).**

## Ranking — time-to-first-small-mission (solo)
