Short decision dossier (Mission 4). **Commercial decisions kept SEPARATE from certification decisions.** Each item:
options · ODG recommendation · what ODG needs. ODG does not decide where a human act is required.

## 1. Price recommendation (justified)
- **Recommendation:** position offers at the **high-value end with recurring** — Offer A **€5–8k**, Offer C **€8–15k
  one-off + €1–2k/mo**. Justification: (a) the €500k model is only solo-feasible at high contract value (see below);
  (b) prices are **market-consistent** (verified: code-audit €4–12k, ISO-readiness €3–6k, ISO-42001 gap $5–15k,
  pentest SMB $8–20k, EuroAIGuard €490–2,290/mo). Still HYPOTHESIS until a quote is accepted.
- **ODG needs:** CTO sign-off on the price band before any proposal is sent.

## 2. Mathematically most-sustainable €500k scenario (with limits)
- **Only the AMBITIOUS high-value+recurring scenario is solo-plausible:** ~€28k/contract (€18k + €2k/mo, conservative
  recurring), **~18 contracts**, ~42 prospects/mo, ~2 signings/mo, 70% gross margin (within the ~120-prospect/4-sign
  solo ceiling). **Prudent (€3k) and central (€9k+€1k/mo) are INFEASIBLE_SOLO** after the V8 conservative-recurring fix.
- **Limits:** HYPOTHESIS conversions unvalidated; cash collected within 12 months is lower (sales+delivery+payment lag);
  client concentration risk (few large contracts); €500k is a **TARGET, not validated demand**.

## 3. Partners / subcontracting need
- At the central/prudent price points, €500k **requires partners/white-label or subcontracting** (capacity beyond a
  solo operator). At the ambitious price point it is borderline solo-feasible. **Recommendation:** pursue the
  high-value path solo first; prepare (not sign) a partner/white-label track for scale — **separate budget authorisation.**
