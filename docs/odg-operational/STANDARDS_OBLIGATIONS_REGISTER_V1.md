## "CU" — AMBIGUOUS, CTO confirmation required (no meaning invented)
Plausible identifiable interpretations: **Common Criteria** (usually "CC"/EAL — possible typo; FR national scheme is
**CSPN/ANSSI**); **Conditions d'Utilisation** (Terms of Use — contractual, not a certification); **EUCC** (EU Common
Criteria scheme, Cybersecurity Act); **SecNumCloud / HDS** (ANSSI cloud-trust / health-data — adjacent if a national
scheme was meant); **CSA STAR**; **"Cas d'Usage" / "Certification Utilisateur"** (internal shorthand). **Conclusion:
"CU" is ambiguous — confirm before any reliance.** Most likely CC/CSPN or Conditions d'Utilisation (both unverified).
