## 1. Demand & trends
- Gartner: 40% of enterprise apps will feature task-specific AI agents by end-2026 (from <5% 2025); agentic AI could reach ~30% of enterprise-app-software revenue (>$450B) by 2035 best-case (press 2025-08-26). [FACT analyst forecast, secondary-read; MED–HIGH]
- Global agentic-AI market commonly cited ~$9.1–10.9B 2026 → ~$139B 2034 ~40% CAGR (aggregators). [FACT estimates exist; LOW–MED, wide dispersion]
- **McKinsey "State of AI 2025" (primary, n=1,993, Jun–Jul 2025):** 62% experimenting with agents; 23% scaling somewhere; ≤10% scaling in any given function; 64% innovation gains, only 39% enterprise EBIT impact (typically <5%). [FACT; HIGH]
- RAG platform market ~$2.0–4.0B 2025; ~$2.0–5.5B 2026, CAGR ~23–49% (firm-dependent). [FACT ranges exist; LOW–MED]
- AI consulting/services ~$9.65–18.6B 2025 (wide); Accenture ~$5.9B gen-AI bookings FY2025 (~2× prior), 6,000+ advanced-AI projects. [market LOW–MED / Accenture FACT HIGH]
- Synthesis [INFERENCE MED]: demand moving from "can we build an agent?" to "can we trust/control/prove an agent in production" — the gap ODG's governance/evidence surface addresses.

## 2. Buyer problems / triggers
- Gartner: **>40% of agentic-AI projects canceled by end-2027** (cost, unclear value, inadequate risk controls); only ~130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors genuinely agentic ("agent washing"). [FACT forecast, secondary-read; MED–HIGH]
- LangChain State of Agent Engineering (n=1,340): **quality #1 production barrier (32%)**; **security #1 concern for large enterprises**; ~30% with prod agents don't evaluate them; 89% some observability, 62% step-level tracing. [FACT vendor survey; MED]
- UC Berkeley MAST (1,600+ traces, 7 frameworks): 14 failure modes; agents "fail quietly." [FACT academic; MED–HIGH]
- OWASP: identity is the central agentic attack surface (3 of top-4 risks identity-focused). [FACT; HIGH]
- Trigger map [INFERENCE MED]: failed/stalled pilot; security/incident review of agent identity/tool-misuse; AI-Act compliance deadline; audit/board request to *prove* an agent behaved within policy — ODG's admission-gate + evidence-provenance + recovery map onto the latter three.
