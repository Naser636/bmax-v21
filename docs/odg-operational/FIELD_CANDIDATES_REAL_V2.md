## Honesty / gaps
- **QUALIFIÉ 1-5** = best exploitable (direct Art.50 exposure, size in ICP, publicly-reasonable decision function).
  **REPÉRÉ 6-10** = public need confirmed but one criterion unresolved (size, HQ-outside-EU, or AI-vendor profile).
- Observable need ≠ purchase intent. URLs sourced by research, **not re-verified** — confirm applicability
  (provider-vs-deployer, size, EU scope) before any contact.
- **Expand the vivier (no fabrication) via:** job posts for CISO/DPO/"AI Act compliance" (Indeed FR, Welcome to the
  Jungle, LinkedIn Jobs) = dated internal need; registries **Pappers/INSEE** (FR) + **Handelsregister** (DE) filtered
  by NAF + headcount ≤750; sector federations (**Numeum, Syntec, AACC**); SaaS product changelogs dating an "AI
  assistant" addition. These would yield 20-40 more candidates with provenance.
