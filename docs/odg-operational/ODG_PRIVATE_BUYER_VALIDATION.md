## 1. ICP (ideal customer profile) — private sector

- Geography: France / EU private companies. Public bodies & tenders excluded.
- Function: finance / accounting-services (cabinets d'expertise comptable) / fintech ops / e-commerce &
  marketplace ops / SaaS billing & revenue ops.
- Size sweet spot: SME to mid-market with enough transaction volume to feel reconciliation pain, small enough to
  lack a bespoke internal tool.
- Must already hold data in **structured** form (CSV/ERP/bank export) — our implemented scope.

## 2. Buy-signal taxonomy (what counts as evidence, and where it is publicly findable)

A buy-signal must be **sourced + dated** from a public source. Acceptable sources:

- Company careers pages / job boards hiring "réconciliation", "rapprochement bancaire", "AP/AR", "contrôle de
  gestion", "comptable" with manual-reconciliation duties.
- Public case studies / blog posts describing reconciliation or close-process pain.
- Fintech/accounting-tech press, funding announcements implying scaling ops.
- Professional communities / forums where practitioners describe the pain (anonymised).

**Not acceptable:** guessed needs, invented budgets, assumed relationships, scraped personal data. Contacts, when
collected, must be **public professional** channels only (company contact form, published pro email, LinkedIn pro
profile) — and still **not contacted** without CTO authorization.

## 3. Qualification scoring (apply to REAL signals once sourced)

Score 0–2 on each; qualify at ≥ 7/10:
1. Pain evidence is explicit & recent (sourced+dated).
2. Data is structured (served by implemented scope) vs needs unstructured extraction (not yet built).
3. Volume/complexity high enough to justify a paid pilot.
4. Reachable via a public professional channel.
5. No obvious blocker (regulated data we can't touch, incumbent tool locked in).

## 6. Qualification questionnaire (discovery call)

1. Quelles sources rapprochez-vous aujourd'hui (grand livre, banque, factures, PSP) et dans quels formats ?
2. Vos données sont-elles **structurées** (export CSV/ERP) ou **non structurées** (PDF/scan) ? (détermine la
   faisabilité immédiate vs roadmap extraction)
3. Volume (lignes/mois) et fréquence du rapprochement ?
4. Comment gérez-vous aujourd'hui les écarts ? Temps passé ? Erreurs trouvées trop tard ?
5. Enjeux d'audit / conformité ?
6. Qui décide ? Budget pour un pilote ? Horizon ?
7. Contraintes données/RGPD (pouvez-vous fournir un échantillon pseudonymisé) ?
