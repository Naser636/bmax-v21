## 1. Demand & trends
- **DORA 2025 (Google Cloud, ~5,000 resp, pub 2025-09-23): 90% of orgs use ≥1 internal developer platform.** [FACT primary; HIGH]
- Gartner: by 2026, 80% of large software-eng orgs will establish platform teams (from 45% 2022). [FACT Gartner prediction; HIGH that it's Gartner's claim]
- IDP/platform-eng market ≈ $10.44bn(2026)→$31.57bn(2031) 24.77% CAGR; NA = 41.68% of 2025 revenue (Mordor). [FACT-as-reported SECONDARY, proprietary; LOW–MED]
- DORA 2024: internal platforms improve individual/team/org performance but **can decrease change stability & throughput** — a real tradeoff requiring developer-independence focus. [FACT primary; HIGH]
- DORA 2025: AI is an "amplifier" — a high-quality internal platform enables AI to magnify org performance; 90% use AI, >80% say it boosts productivity, 30% little/no trust in AI-generated code. [FACT primary; HIGH]
- ~76% have a dedicated platform team (secondary summaries; not in the primary excerpt). [INFERENCE MED]
- Synthesis [INFERENCE MED]: near-universal "we have a platform," but maturity/ROI lag — adoption-vs-value gap; the 2024→2025 DORA shift ("falling short" → "AI amplifier/enabler") = market moving from *do we build one?* to *does ours reduce load + work with AI?*

## 2. Buyer problems / triggers
Developer cognitive load (Team Topologies — platform teams absorb extraneous load) [FACT concept HIGH]; golden paths / paved roads [FACT concept HIGH]; Thinnest Viable Platform (guard against over-building) [HIGH]; deployment-velocity-vs-stability tension (DORA: IDPs can raise productivity while lowering stability if done poorly) [FACT HIGH]; DIY failure claims ("~70% of platform teams fail / ~half disbanded in 18mo"; "40–50% cognitive-load reduction") [HYPOTHESIS, unverified primary; LOW]. Triggers [INFERENCE MED]: scaling past ~50–100 engineers; a failed DIY Backstage build; a cloud-cost shock (cost-aware golden paths); a regulatory/audit driver (NIS2/SecNumCloud/CRA evidence); AI-coding rollout exposing that AI without a solid platform amplifies dysfunction.
