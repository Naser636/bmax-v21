# ODG — Client-value model (V1, 2026-10-27)

Combinations of offers A (audit) + C (assurance) + recurring, modelled WITHOUT applying any price commercially. All
prices HYPOTHESIS until CTO-validated + market-proven. Grounded in verified comparables (COMMERCIAL_RESEARCH_V3) +
channel economics (`revenue-channels`). No client/result/revenue invented; 0 engagements.

## Value ladder (land-and-expand — pattern, not demand)
1. **Paid pilot** (fixed scope, budget-holder-funded) — fastest first cash (SALES_CYCLE_RESEARCH_V1); low trust bar.
2. **Main project** (audit A / assurance C) with measurable acceptance criteria.
3. **Post-audit remediation** — pre-qualified by the audit's findings.
4. **Recurring monitoring/assurance** — highest trust bar; sell as expansion, not entry.
5. **Partner / white-label** — borrowed credibility; scale channel.
Evidence: paid pilots signed by the budget holder convert ~60–90% to the larger deal (arcshift); decisive variable = who signs.

## Pricing structures (HYPOTHESIS; margins conservative)
| structure | price (HYP) | gross margin | churn/dependency risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid pilot (fixed) | €1.5–3k | high | one-off; risk = pilot disguised as full project |
| Main project (acceptance-gated) | A €5–8k / C €8–15k | ~70% | concentration if few large deals |
| Recurring (after pilot accepted) | €1–2k/mo | ~70% | **churn** (5% model → recurring decays); dependency on retention |
| Partner / white-label | commission 20–40% | net ~40% | dependency on partner relationship + their pipeline |

## Combinations (reuse `revenue-channels`)
- **Pilot → project → recurring** (one account): best margin path; recurring compounds but churns; cash lags (V9).
- **Project + partner volume**: reaches higher gross cash (€489k @30% commission) but net margin ~40%.
- **Project + subcontract capacity**: highest gross (€651k) but 30% net margin + working-capital to pay subcontractors.
See `PATHS_TO_500K_V1` for the figures.

## Unit-economics discipline
Per engagement track: signed value, invoiced, cashed (lag-aware), direct cost (time), channel cost (if any), gross
margin. **Do not book theoretical revenue that exceeds delivery capacity (≤3 concurrent) or whose cash lands after the
horizon.** Signed ≠ invoiced ≠ cashed ≠ margin ≠ net.

## Risks to value-per-client
Concentration (few high-value clients), churn on recurring, delivery capacity ceiling, payment lag/non-payment, partner
dependency. Mitigation: a **mix** of pilot + project + recurring across several accounts, not a single big contract.

## Honesty
No offer is sold as operational beyond proven capability; recurring is modelled with churn, not perpetual; partner
economics reduce margin. Nothing validated; prices HYPOTHESIS; no commitment without CTO.
