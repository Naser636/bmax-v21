## 2. Company-size commercial archetypes (Phase 7) — private sector, prices HYPOTHESIS
Offer shape scales with buyer size. **All figures are hypothesis ranges for design only — not quotes, not validated willingness-to-pay.**
| Band | Profile | Offer archetype | Best-fit professions | Price posture (HYPOTHESIS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Why these fits:** the forcing-function professions (P3 CRA, P8 e-invoicing, P10 AI-Act) reach **down to micro/small** because the mandates are universal; the capability-depth professions (P2 agentic governance, P6 eval-integrity, P7 reliability, P9 FinOps) fit **ETI/large** where budgets and complexity live. This mirrors the Phase-2 buyer-by-size findings exactly.

## 3. Entry wedge (honest sequencing)
The ranking (Phase 2) + qualification state (§1) + size fit (§2) converge on a **private-sector entry wedge**: lead with **P3 (CRA/SBOM)** and **P8 (France e-invoicing)** — hard 2026/2027 deadlines, universal buyer base incl. micro/small, all modules already merged (POC-OWN), fastest plausible time-to-first-deliverable. **P2 and P10** follow (AI-Act clock, strong capability). This wedge is a *plan*, not a pipeline — advancing any wedge to QUALIFIED is owner-gated (requires authorized buyer contact ODG does not have).

## 4. What this explicitly does NOT claim
No signed client · no revenue · no validated price · no outbound contact made · no profession above POC-OWN/R3 · no public-sector pursuit · P5/O8 remains SPEC-ONLY R1 with no IDP. Commercial success ≠ customer readiness ≠ validation ≠ implementation ≠ research (the mandate's firewall, preserved).
