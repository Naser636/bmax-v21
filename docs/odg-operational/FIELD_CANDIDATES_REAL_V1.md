## C. The realistic BUYER profile (where the real list must be built)
The strongest buyers are **NOT** the AI-native funded startups above (they have in-house governance) but **EU SMEs/
SMCs (≤750 emp, ≤€150M rev)** that have **added an LLM/generative feature** and now face, with no in-house AI/legal
team: **Art. 50 transparency (since 2026-08-02)** and the **technical-marking duty for generative/synthetic content by
2026-12-02** (artificialintelligenceact.eu; million-marketing.fr). This cohort is **not enumerable via US-only
WebSearch** — it requires registry-level sourcing (see method below). **Honest limitation: no verified SME buyer list is
produced here.**
