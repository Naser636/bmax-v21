## 3. Public-tender feasibility (honest)
- **PLACE** (marches-publics.gouv.fr) + **APProch** (DAE) — official, **free**, SIRET-based registration.
- **APProch publishes forecasts (prévisionnels), NOT contracts/live tenders** — an intention to maybe buy later.
- **Solo/micro eligibility:** legally **YES** (Code de la commande publique; references not legally required). **But practically disadvantaged:** buyers may set proportionate minima (turnover, 3-yr accounts, insurance, sometimes certifications) a brand-new entity lacks; micro turnover ceiling (€77,700 services, BOFiP 2026) caps volume. **Verdict:** registration is a legit €0 step; near-term awards unrealistic for a new entity; no open tender for these services verified.
