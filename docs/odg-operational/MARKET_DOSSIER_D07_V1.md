## 1. Demand & trends
- **Observability market SIZE — wide dispersion by definition; no single authoritative figure.** "Observability tools & platforms" ~$28B 2025→~164B 2035 ~19% (Market Research Future); $28.5B→172.1B 19.7% (Research Nester); narrower "observability market" ~$3.5–4.8B 2025 (Dimension/DataIntelo). - **OpenTelemetry = 2nd-most-active CNCF project** (CNCF 2024 report); 89% invest in Prometheus, 85% in OpenTelemetry, ~40% both (Grafana 2024 survey). [FACT MED–HIGH] — open standards erode pure-telemetry lock-in.
- **DORA 2024 (>39,000 resp):** added 5th metric (rework rate); high-performer cluster 31%→22%, low cluster 17%→25%; docs + psych-safety top reliability predictors; +25% AI-adoption ≈ −7.2% delivery stability. [FACT HIGH]
- **SRE-as-a-Service** ~$1.3B(2024)→$6.7B(2033) 20.1% (driver = complexity + SRE talent shortage → managed SRE). [FACT-as-reported SECONDARY; LOW–MED]
- Addressable demand [INFERENCE MED]: the durable signal is **reliability discipline + incident evidence + SLO governance**, not more telemetry — supported by OTel/Prometheus commoditizing collection, DORA's stability regression under AI, and regulatory incident-reporting (§7). Not WTP.

## 2. Buyer problems / triggers
Downtime cost: ITIC 2024 (>1,000 firms) — a single hour >$300k for >90% of mid/large enterprises; 41% report $1M–5M/hr; finance peaks ~$9.3M/hr; EMA ~$14,056/min (~$843k/hr); CrowdStrike Jul-2024 ~$5.4B combined F500 loss [FACT MED–HIGH, survey self-report wide variance]. **Alert fatigue = #1 obstacle to faster incident response** by ~2:1 (Grafana 2025 survey, primary, n=1,255, pub 2026-03-26) [FACT HIGH headline]. Granular alert-fatigue stats (77%≥10 alerts/day; 57% <30% actionable; 44% outage from ignored alerts; 78% incident w/ no alert) — secondary-reported, not in primary [MED, individually unconfirmed]. MTTR worsening: only 9%(2024) vs 14%(2023) greatly reduced MTTR; ~60% critical MTTR 30min–2hr [FACT MED self-report]. Triggers [INFERENCE MED]: a costly outage/post-mortem; a NIS2/DORA incident-reporting obligation; board pressure on reliability; an SLO/error-budget initiative stalling on "can't trust our numbers"; alert-noise burnout.

## 6. Procurement / trust / security
Regulated buyers require operational-resilience evidence (DORA register of information, incident classification/reporting, annual resilience testing, ICT third-party oversight) [FACT HIGH]; open standards (OTel/Prometheus) de-risk procurement (anti-lock-in) [INFERENCE MED]; ODG's audit-chain/evidence-provenance/fail-visible-health aligns with "show me the evidence" procurement, but ODG has no certification (no COFRAC/ISO SATISFIED claimed) [FACT].

## 7. EU/FR + international regulatory
**NIS2 (Dir. 2022/2555) Art.23 three-stage incident reporting:** 24h early warning, 72h notification, 1-month final report, clock from awareness; trust-service providers 24h; runs independently of GDPR's 72h [FACT HIGH, EUR-Lex not directly fetched]. **DORA (Reg. 2022/2554):** applies since 2025-01-17; ~22,000 EU financial entities; ICT risk frameworks, major-incident classification+reporting, annual resilience testing, ICT third-party oversight, Register of Information [FACT HIGH, Central Bank of Ireland + corroboration]. France: leading SRE-platform adoption in BFSI/health/manufacturing (secondary) [LOW–MED]; NIS2+DORA apply to FR regulated entities. International: US reliability discipline anchored by DORA metrics / Google SRE canon, not regulation [FACT HIGH]. Inference [MED]: NIS2/DORA convert "reliability + incident evidence" from nice-to-have into a *compliance obligation* for EU regulated entities — the strongest structural tailwind for ODG's incident-evidence-integrity angle (not WTP).

## 8. Gaps & differentiation (INFERENCE MED)
Market gap: big vendors sell collection + dashboards; buyers still report untrustworthy metrics, alert noise, rising MTTR, incident-record integrity as unmet; OTel/Prometheus commoditize the data layer. Under-served layer = **provenanced reliability assurance + incident-evidence integrity + SLO governance** — exactly ODG's honesty contract (survivorship refusal, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, mechanism≠satisfied, PREPARE-not-APPLY). ODG differentiation = a *reliability-evidence auditor/governor* that refuses to fabricate a green number — a complement *beside* Datadog/Grafana/OTel data, not a replacement. Hard limit: ODG's evidence today is over its OWN runs; applying it to a customer's prod telemetry is unbuilt.
