A **pre-audit AI-readiness assurance service**: determine honestly, from executed checks + cited sources, whether an AI system's evidence supports a readiness claim — refusing to certify, refusing to call declared evidence "verified", reporting `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`/`NO_DATA`. IN: AI-Act role & obligation triage, ISO/IEC 42001 pre-audit gap analysis, requirement→control→evidence dossiers, agentic-AI governance review. ## E. Discovery, intake & diagnostic process
