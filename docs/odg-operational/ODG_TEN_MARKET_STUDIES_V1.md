# ODG Ten Private-Sector Market Studies + Ranking (V1)

**CTO Master Campaign V2, Phase 2.** Date **2026-11-01**. Ten **private-sector-only** market studies (France / Europe / international) for professions P1–P10, scored on a **100-point weighted rubric** with a consolidated ranking. **Scope: private companies only — public-sector/government procurement is EXCLUDED as a customer** (private regulated-sector firms — banks, insurers, health, energy — are in scope). Market sizes are third-party analyst estimates with large inter-firm dispersion; **every market-size / CAGR figure is labelled FACT** (sourced 2024–2026) **or INFERENCE** (a few buyer-share / adoption percentages drawn from the same cited sources are stated inline without a separate tag). **Scores are INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS for prioritization — NOT validated revenue, pipeline, or client proof (ODG has €0 clients, no profession R4+).** Fresh research captured by three independent analyst agents (2026-11-01); sources inline.
