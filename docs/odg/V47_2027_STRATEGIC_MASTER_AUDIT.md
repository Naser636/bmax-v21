## Conclusion (strategic)
- **Do NOT build blind.** The 2027 priority is **demand proof + commercial machine**, not more technical construction. The single highest-leverage P0 gap is a **reference client** (unlocks everything up-market).
- **€5M-2027 is a target to earn, not a plan of record.** The realistic standing-start range is ~€0.5–1.5M in 2027; €5M needs early capital + team + channel + references.
- **Highest-potential wedge for scale:** **P3 + P10 enterprise compliance programs** (CRA + AI-Act + ISO), €100k+ tickets, in the $11–39B DevSecOps/GRC markets — the only mix that reaches €5M without 700 small deals.
- **Next research step (not construction):** size the 12 remaining métiers' demand, validate 3 prices, and design the enterprise+channel go-to-market — then (later mission) a technical roadmap to close the P0/P1 gaps.
