# V45.1 — €500k NET-PROFIT financial model (FR SAS + Morocco team) — 2026-11-10

> CTO Directive V45.1 §4. **Objective = €500,000 NET PROFIT (résultat net après IS) retained in the SAS, after ALL expenses and corporate tax — NOT revenue.** Honesty rules: no inflated margins, no understated costs, €500k is **not** presented as achievable by default. All external figures are **directional, dated, and REQUIRE confirmation** before any commitment. FX assumption: **1 EUR ≈ 10.7 MAD** (verify). Sources at the end.

## 0. Tax & cost anchors (verified 2026-11-10, confirm before use)
- **French IS 2026:** 15% up to €42,500 of profit (eligible PME: CA < €10M, capital ≥75% held by individuals), **25% above** ([Legalstart](https://www.legalstart.fr/fiches-pratiques/fiscalite-entreprises/taux-is/), [propulse by CA](https://propulsebyca.fr/impots-et-taxes/impot-societes)).
- **Dividend distribution (owner personal):** PFU "flat tax" **30%** (12.8% IR + 17.2% prélèvements sociaux) — well-established FR regime.
- **Morocco dev salaries (MAD/month):** junior 8–14k, mid 14–24k, senior 24–40k (specialist to 55k) ([Xpertize](https://xpertize.africa/en/articles/2026-07-10-morocco-software-developer-salary), [SalaryExpert](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/software-engineer/morocco)).
- **Morocco CNSS employer charges:** headline **~21.09%**, but the long/short-term branches (8.98%) are **capped at a 6,000 MAD base**; uncapped portion (family 6.40% + AMO 4.11% + VTT 1.60% = 12.11%) applies to full salary ⇒ **effective employer uplift ≈ 13–16%** for mid/senior ([Upsilon](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/en/employer-social-charges-morocco/), [Upsilon CNSS 2026](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/en/cnss-contributions-morocco-2026/)).

## 1. The €500k-NET arithmetic (the number that matters)
To retain **€500,000 net after IS**, with the 15%/25% bands:
- Net = 0.75 × EBT + 4,250 (the €4,250 = the 10% saving on the first €42,500). Set = 500,000 ⇒ **EBT (résultat avant impôt) ≈ €661,000**; **IS ≈ €161,000**.
- **Required revenue = EBT + total operating costs.** So the revenue needed depends entirely on the cost base (team + overhead) below.

## 4. C — Revenue feasibility check (the honest constraint)
From the market study pricing (verify): P3/P11 **entry audit €5–15k**, **recurring €1–3k/mo**, **integration €8–25k**. To reach **~€971k revenue** mostly from TPE/PME:
- e.g. **60 audits @ €8k (€480k)** + **30 integrations @ €12k (€360k)** + **recurring €130k** ≈ €970k.
- That implies **~90 closed B2B deals in year 1** + a recurring base — requiring a **real sales + delivery team and a pipeline that itself takes 3–6 months to build**. **Not realistic in month 1–12 from a standing start.**

## 5. SAS net profit ≠ owner personal cash
- **€500k net in the SAS** is the company result. If distributed to the owner as dividends: **−30% PFU ⇒ ~€350k personal**; retained in the SAS ⇒ €500k stays as equity/trésorerie (distributable later).
- Owner may instead take **salary** (deductible expense, lowers EBT, incurs FR social charges ~ high) — a different optimization; modeled here as dividends for simplicity. **The €500k objective is a company metric; personal take-home after distribution ≈ €350k.**

## 5b. Multi-horizon to €500k NET (18 / 24 / 30 months) — target not abandoned
€500k NET is an annual **run-rate**, not a month-1 outcome. Realistic path to that run-rate:
- **By month 18:** Central run-rate (~€300k net / ~€577k revenue, team ~6). €500k not yet reached.
- **By month 24:** approaching Ambitious if ≥1 reference client + channel exist (team ~8). €500k net run-rate plausible only with ~€971k revenue secured.
- **By month 30:** Ambitious run-rate (~€971k revenue, team ~10) ⇒ €500k NET achievable **if** acquisition scaled to ~90 deals/yr + recurring.
- **Cumulative vs run-rate:** cumulative net over the first 12–18 months is far below €500k (ramp + hiring + CAC come first); the €500k is a steady-state annual figure, reached ~month 24–30, not before.

## 5c. Working capital, VAT, cash (not profit)
- **VAT (TVA 20%):** collected on sales, deducted on purchases, remitted — **cash timing only, not a cost**; but it affects **trésorerie** (the SAS fronts VAT between invoice and remittance).
- **Working capital (BFR):** B2B payment terms 30–60 days ⇒ the SAS funds salaries (Morocco, monthly) **before** client cash arrives — a real cash gap at ramp; needs a buffer ≈ 2–3 months of opex (~€30–80k) or it blocks hiring.
- **Recruitment/CAC are cash-upfront, revenue-lagging** ⇒ the first months are cash-negative even at a good margin.

## 6. Honest verdict
- **€500k NET after IS in year 1 is NOT realistic** from a standing start: it needs **~€971k revenue (~90 PME deals + recurring)** supported by a **~10-person team** that must be hired and ramped first, and a pipeline that doesn't exist yet (**€0 clients, L2 demonstrators**).
- **Realistic trajectory:** **Prudent ~€120k net (year 1)** → **Central ~€300k net (year ~2)** → **Ambitieux €500k net (year ~2–3)**, conditioned on: (a) ≥1 reference client, (b) a repeatable P3/P11 offer with verified pricing, (c) a built Morocco delivery team, (d) a working acquisition channel.
- **€500k NET is the 18–30-month objective, not the year-1 plan of record.** Reported as such, not as a guarantee.

## 7. Risks & success conditions
- **Acquisition is the binding constraint** (not delivery): ~90 deals/yr needs marketing spend + a sales function + proof (reference client). Top risk = overclaiming L2 as production-ready.
- **FX/tax/salary figures are directional** — confirm IS eligibility (capital held ≥75% by individuals), real Morocco loaded costs, and competitor prices (3 quotes) before committing.
- **France–Morocco structure** must stay compliant (permanent-establishment risk if the SAS effectively operates from Morocco; a Moroccan entity or EoR may be required — legal review needed; see RH model).
- **Success condition for €500k net:** team built + ≥1 reference + repeatable offer + channel + ~€1M revenue run-rate. Absent these, plan to the Prudent/Central band and report honestly.
