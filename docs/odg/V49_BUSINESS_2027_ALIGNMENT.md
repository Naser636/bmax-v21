# V49 — World excellence ↔ 2027 business (€5M) alignment

> Does world-class excellence move ODG toward 2027 revenue? Mapped to acquisition / credibility / delivery / automation / margin / recurrence / scale, and to the V47.1 priority métiers (P3, P9, P10, P11, P15).

## Where excellence helps 2027 (and where it does not)
- **CREDIBILITY (biggest commercial lever of excellence):** NASA IV&V, Airbus safety-cases, NIST RMF, ISO/ENISA cert-readiness → these make ODG's offers **enterprise-trustworthy** (the P9/P10/P3 enterprise programs that reach the €5M mix need exactly this). **Pursue certification-readiness packaging.**
- **DELIVERY + AUTOMATION:** Toyota lean, SpaceX iterate, DeepMind eval, Amazon DORA → keep delivery cost low + quality high (the ~8× automation dividend). Supports margin at scale.
- **RECURRENCE/SCALE:** Siemens automation, scientific knowledge-mgmt → support recurring + scale, secondary for 2027.
- **ACQUISITION: NONE of the excellence methods generate demand.** As V48 found, the revenue blocker is commercial (reference client, sales motion, channel) — excellence raises CREDIBILITY which *helps convert* enterprise buyers, but does not create the pipeline.

## Link to V47.1 priority métiers
- **P10 (AI-Gov) + P3 (Security):** NIST RMF + ISO 42001/27001 + CRA cert-readiness = directly sellable enterprise credibility.
- **P9 (FinOps):** Amazon Well-Architected + DORA cost discipline = the ROI story buyers fund.
- **P11 (e-invoice) + P15 (automation):** Toyota/Siemens process rigor = reliable delivery.

## Verdict
**World-class excellence is necessary for the ENTERPRISE/€100k+ programs in the €5M mix (credibility), but not sufficient — it must be paired with the commercial arm (V48 I1).** Technical excellence ≠ market demand ≠ sales closed.
