# V51 — Canonical financial objective + feasibility (€500,000,000 NET, first 12 months)

> CTO V51 §2/§3. **Official objective (corrected, canonical): €500,000,000 NET PROFIT after ALL charges and taxes, in the FIRST 12 MONTHS.** NET = after salaries/charges, subcontracting, acquisition/marketing, infra/software, insurance/legal/admin, delivery/support, financing, and taxes. **NOT revenue, NOT pre-tax, NOT multi-year.** Prior targets (€500k, €5M) are superseded as the objective and kept only as historical scenarios (never as validated forecasts). Honesty: the objective is NOT lowered; its feasibility is assessed truthfully.

## Arithmetic (verified)
- €500M NET after FR IS (25%) ⇒ **required pre-tax profit (EBT) ≈ €666.7M**, **IS ≈ €166.7M**.
- Required **revenue** by net margin: **25% ⇒ ~€2.0B · 15% ⇒ ~€3.3B · 10% ⇒ ~€5.0B** in year 1.
- Required **deals/year** (revenue ÷ avg deal): @€120k enterprise ⇒ **~16,700–41,700** (~1,400–3,500/month); @€25k ⇒ **80,000–200,000**; @€8k ⇒ **250,000–625,000**.

## Starting reality (V47–V50, verified)
- **0 paying clients. 0 observed conversion. 0 commercial references.** Organisation ≈ owner + ODG. Capabilities L2/L3 (5 métiers E4-demonstrated, synthetic). No sales team, no channel, no capital line, no BFR buffer.

## Feasibility verdict (explicit, not fabricated)
**INFEASIBLE in the stated conditions and timeframe — by roughly three orders of magnitude (~1000×).**
- €500M NET/year ≈ the **annual net profit of a LARGE established IT-services multinational** (tens of thousands of staff, billions in revenue, decades of references). It is **not** a quantity a 0-client entity reaches in 12 months.
- Required revenue (~€2–5B) exceeds the demonstrated reachable 2027 range (~€0.5–1.5M, V45.3/V47.1) by **~1,000–3,000×**.
- Delivering ~€2–5B of services needs **thousands of FTE** and a global acquisition engine — neither exists nor is buildable in 12 months from €0.
- **Conclusion:** the €500M-net-in-12-months objective **cannot be demonstrated as achievable** from the current situation. Per directive §3, the target is **NOT replaced by a lower figure** — it is recorded as the stated objective with an explicit infeasibility finding.

## What would have to change for €500M NET to become conceivable (not in 12 months)
1. **Not a standing start:** acquisition of/merger with an already-large revenue base (€2–5B), OR many years of compounding.
2. **Capital:** hundreds of millions invested (team, acquisition, BFR) before any €500M net.
3. **Workforce:** thousands of delivery + sales staff (ODG automation lowers per-deal cost but cannot remove the acquisition/relationship labour at this scale).
4. **References + brand:** enterprise trust at multinational scale (certifications, track record).
5. **Timeframe:** realistically a **multi-year to decade** trajectory, not 12 months — which the directive's "first 12 months" excludes.

## Honest framing for the Boussole
- **Objective (canonical, recorded):** €500M NET / first 12 months.
- **Assessment:** INFEASIBLE from the current €0-client state in 12 months (shown above). The plausible 12-month range remains ~€0.5–1.5M revenue (far below €500M net). This is stated as a FACT-based finding, not an adjustment of the target.
- Technical excellence (V12.4) and the E4 deliverables improve delivery/credibility but **do not change this feasibility conclusion** — the gap is commercial scale + capital + time, not technique.
